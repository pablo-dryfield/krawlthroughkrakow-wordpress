The Meeting Point

For the regular Krakow pub crawl, meet at 9 PM by Adam Mickiewicz Monument (Adam Statue) in Krakow's Main Market Square. Look for the guides and their pink umbrella.

Check your booking confirmation before heading out. You can find joining information and bookable regular-crawl dates online.

New Year's Eve is a separate event: groups start at 5 PM or 7:30 PM, and meeting points are to be announced. Follow the NYE event information, not this regular 9 PM meeting.
