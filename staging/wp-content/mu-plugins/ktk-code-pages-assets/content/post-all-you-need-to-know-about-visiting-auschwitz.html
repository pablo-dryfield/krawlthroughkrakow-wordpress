Everything you need to know about visiting Auschwitz

by Jess Campbell | Sep 5, 2019 | Uncategorized

A visit to the Auschwitz-Birkenau Memorial deserves time and preparation. This guide explains entry passes, transport from Krakow and practical arrangements, with links to the Memorial's own information.

Visitor information reviewed 14 November 2026. Availability and arrangements can change; check the official information for your date.

How far is Auschwitz from Krakow?

The Memorial is in Oświęcim, west of Krakow. Plan your route to the Visitor Services Centre at 55 Więźniów Oświęcimia Street, where visits begin at the former Auschwitz I site. Use the Memorial's transport information to check the destination, rather than relying on a general town-centre stop.

Choose an arrival with room for delays and the recommended 30-minute check-in margin before your booked tour. A travel estimate is not a guaranteed arrival time.

View the Memorial's current directions and entrance map

How much does it cost to go to Auschwitz?

A personalized entry pass is required for both Auschwitz I and Auschwitz II-Birkenau. Individual visitors must reserve online through the Memorial's official reservation system; passes are not available at the entrance.

Unguided visits have limited free passes at designated times. Guided visits are paid. Check the current visit options for language, availability and price. Free admission does not mean you can arrive without a reservation, and a transport ticket alone is not an entry pass.

How do I get to Auschwitz?

You can plan independent travel or choose a package with transport. Match the outbound journey to your reserved entry time, check the exact arrival stop and arrange the return journey too.

Auschwitz I and Auschwitz II-Birkenau are about 3.5 km apart. The Museum provides a shuttle between them; check the arrangements for your visit. The official directions also explain the visitor centre, station and parking locations.

Bus to Auschwitz

The Memorial links to Lajkonik services from Krakow to its car park. Consult the operator's Krakow-Oświęcim timetable for the current route and stops, including Krakow MDA at Bosacka 18.

Confirm the service date, fare, boarding point, museum stop and return journey with the operator before booking. Allow for traffic rather than choosing a connection that arrives at the last minute.

Train to Auschwitz

For rail travel, look for a connection to Oświęcim on your chosen date. The railway station is about 1.5 km from the Visitor Services Centre, according to the Museum's directions.

Include that final transfer in your arrival plan. Check current rail services, fares and the return journey with the operator, and leave enough time between the train's arrival and your Museum entry slot.

Tour company to Auschwitz

A package can make travel arrangements simpler, but compare the written details before paying. Check whether it includes a named entry pass, a Museum guide-educator, transport between the two sites and the pickup and return arrangements you need.

Ask the operator to confirm the entry date, time, language and what happens if the arrangements change. Do not treat a low package price or a transport reservation as proof of Museum admission. Use the official visit information to understand the available visit types.

How long do you need to visit Auschwitz?

A standard guided visit takes approximately 3.5 hours. Add travel from Krakow, check-in, transfers and time for a break; the tour duration is not the total length of your day.

Leave room in your plans afterwards rather than rushing to another reservation. For other formats and availability, consult the Memorial's visit options.

Is Auschwitz open all year round?

The Memorial's ordinary closure dates are 1 January, 25 December and Easter Sunday. Seasonal entrance hours vary, and temporary closures are possible. The published entrance schedule begins at 7:30 AM.

Check the official opening hours and your actual entry pass before travelling. Opening hours are not a promise of an available pass at every time, or permission to arrive at a different time from your reservation.

Is there food available to buy at Auschwitz?

Plan meals, water and breaks as part of your travel day. Check current facilities and ask the Visitor Services Centre where you can stop for a break; do not assume refreshments are included in a transport package.

Follow the Museum's visitor guidance and on-site instructions about what you may bring and where eating is permitted.

Can I take photos at Auschwitz?

The Museum generally permits personal photographs without flash or stands, with restrictions including the victims' hair room in Block 4 and the basements of Block 11. Follow signs and staff instructions; commercial photography needs prior approval.

Read the current photography guidance and respect the dignity of the place and other visitors.

Is anything not permitted in Auschwitz?

Check the visitor rules before packing. The stated bag limit is 35 x 25 x 15 cm. Paid storage is available; check current arrangements rather than relying on an old locker price. Keep your named pass ready and check which identity or concession documents you need.

The Memorial advises against visits by children under 14. If you have access requirements, review its accessibility information before choosing arrangements. Historic buildings and surfaces can be difficult to navigate.
