Planning a student group trip

Use our student-trip enquiry page to discuss your dates, expected numbers and preferred activities. Minimum group sizes, private arrangements, booking lead times and deposit or cancellation terms are confirmed individually in each quote.

Do not assume that an old accommodation-and-transfer bundle, fixed price or free organiser place is available now. Ask for the current arrangements in writing before making plans for the group.
