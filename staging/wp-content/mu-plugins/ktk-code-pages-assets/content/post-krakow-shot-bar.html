Food or an activity can change the evening

The Herring Embassy and Cosmic Minigolf Pub also remain KTK recommendations. Check The Herring Embassy for its current food and drink offering, and Cosmic Minigolf Pub for activity booking and admission. Do not assume that ordering a drink includes a game, that a kitchen stays open with the bar or that a particular match will be screened.

Late-night snacks: check the branch and kitchen

KTK recommends Banialuka, but a venue name is not proof of 24-hour service. Use its operator site for the branch and current menu, then confirm food service separately from bar hours. Our budget-drinks guide provides dated comparisons instead of an all-day-price guarantee.

Know the price and keep control of the plan

Ask the price and serving size before ordering, check the bill and keep your drink in sight. Official Poland travel advice covers drink and payment precautions. These apply to nightlife generally; a KTK recommendation is not a safety guarantee.

Adult-entertainment venues: do not assume the terms

Make your own choice without pressure from street promoters or anyone in your group. Understand entry, drink and service charges before agreeing or paying. We do not label an entire category of businesses criminal or promise that another category is automatically safe.

Keep permanent decisions separate

Do not let a night out or pressure from friends become the reason for a permanent decision. Arrange a separate tattoo appointment and discuss the provider's conditions directly rather than adding an impulsive stop to a bar route.

Old Town and Kazimierz: choose the actual location

Compare the address with your next stop and accommodation. Leave time to eat and travel, and decide where the group will meet if you split up. A nearby bar is not necessarily part of a guided tour's itinerary.

Pijalnia: branches are separate choices

The operator's location list includes Rynek Glowny 9, Plac Nowy 7, Szewska 20 and Stolarska 5 in Krakow. Choose the actual branch and check its menu and hours. The list does not establish that every branch has the same offer or stays open around the clock.

A good evening does not need a drinking target

Choose the bar for your group's preferences, agree the budget and leave room to stop or change the plan.
