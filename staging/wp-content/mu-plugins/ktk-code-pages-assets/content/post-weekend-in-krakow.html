Weekend In Krakow: Partying And Other 15 Top Things To Do

by DPBowman | Jun 29, 2020 | Krakow Guides, Party Tips

A flexible weekend in Krakow

Use these 15 daytime ideas and a night out to build your own weekend, not as a promise to fit every ticketed attraction into two days. Keep the Old Town stops together, allow a separate stretch for Kazimierz and Podgorze, and leave room for meals, queues and the journey home.

Sightseeing: group nearby stops together

1. Main Market Square

Start around the Main Market Square and the Cloth Hall, then decide which interiors you want to visit rather than buying every ticket. Keep your evening meeting instructions separate from daytime sightseeing: How to Join has the current KTK arrangements.

2. A Planty walk

Use part of the Planty park for a break around the Old Town. Choose a short section that fits your route instead of feeling you need to complete a whole circuit.

3. St Mary's Basilica

While you are around the square, decide whether to include a visitor tour of St Mary's Basilica. Check the parish's visitor information for the tourist entrance, admission and hours; services and tourist visits have different arrangements.

4. Wawel Castle and the hill

Choose whether you want an outdoor look around or a particular exhibition. Check Wawel's official visitor information for the route, entrance time and tickets. Castle exhibitions and the cathedral have separate arrangements; do not assume access to every interior comes with a walk on the hill.

5. A Vistula-side walk

After Wawel, a riverside section can give the day a slower pace before another museum or a meal. Pick the distance around your group and the weather, keep clear of cycle traffic and leave enough time to get to any reserved entry.

6. Kazimierz

Give Kazimierz its own part of the day rather than treating it as a quick detour. The Galicia Jewish Museum's heritage routes are a useful starting point for understanding the area; check admission and visiting arrangements for individual sites.

7. Podgorze and Ghetto Heroes Square

Ghetto Heroes Square is in Podgorze, across the river, not in Kazimierz. If you include it, allow time for that distinct area and approach memorial sites respectfully. Do not confuse a walk through the neighbourhood with a booked museum visit.

8. Town Hall Tower

This is another option around the Main Market Square, not a compulsory extra after every other museum. Check the museum's current tower page for admission, opening and access information. Choose it around your interests and ability to manage the visit rather than relying on an old description of the upper floors.

Food and drink: leave time between plans

9. A pierogi meal

Make room for a proper meal and compare fillings, portions and toppings before ordering. Sharing several types can suit a group, but ask about ingredients and dietary requirements individually. Our food guide has more starting ideas; check the venue's current menu.

10. Okraglak and Plac Nowy

Plac Nowy in Kazimierz is a convenient place to look for a zapiekanka around the central Okraglak building. Compare the stalls' actual menus and portions. Do not assume the same vendor, toppings or queue will be there at every hour, or that one person's favourite must be everyone's.

11. A food-truck stop

Food trucks can offer different choices within one group. Choose a current location near your route and check who is serving that day, so you can compare menus without spending the afternoon travelling between vendors. Leave enough time for food before joining the pub crawl, rather than ordering just before a scheduled start.

12. An organised tasting or activity

If a guided tasting suits your group, compare the current Polish vodka tasting with other day activities. Read the inclusions, tasting format, date and joining instructions before booking, and check whether food is included or should be arranged separately. For more inspiration, browse the existing things-to-do articles.

Shopping: choose a useful stop, not another checklist

13. Galeria Krakowska

If shopping or an indoor break fits your plans, check Galeria Krakowska's operator site for shops and current hours, including your particular Sunday or holiday. It is on Pawia Street by the main station. Do not build a tight departure around an assumed opening time or an old shop count.

14. The Cloth Hall

Combine a look at the Cloth Hall's trading area with the Main Market Square rather than making a separate cross-city journey. Compare souvenirs before buying. Trading stalls, the upstairs gallery and Rynek Underground are different experiences with their own visiting arrangements.

15. Nowy Kleparz

For a market alternative, check the Nowy Kleparz operator's information and choose a visit around the traders and products you want. Nowy Kleparz is distinct from Stary Kleparz; save the correct location rather than assuming the names refer to the same market. Ask before photographing people or stalls.

16. A Krakow night out

Choose an evening that fits your group and the following day's plans. Our nightlife guide helps compare bars, music and clubs; check the actual event and admission conditions.

For an organised social night, read Krawl Through Krakow's joining information and check the current pub-crawl booking page. The regular crawl is for ages 18 and over; a valid ID is recommended. Keep time for dinner and your return transport, and avoid booking an early departure against an evening you have not planned.
