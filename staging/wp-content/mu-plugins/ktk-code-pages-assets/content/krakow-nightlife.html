Krakow nightlife: where to go after dark

A good night out in Krakow starts with the kind of evening you actually want: a conversation over drinks, a club programme worth planning around, or a group to explore with. Start with the Old Town around the Main Market Square, then choose a few places rather than trying to fit the whole city into one night.

Choose your starting point:

  • Clubs: check the actual event, door time and admission conditions for your date.

Where to start: Old Town or Kazimierz?

The Old Town is a useful base if you want several central venues within the same area. Our recommendations include places on Szewska, Sławkowska, Szpitalna and Bracka, as well as around the Main Market Square. Check the exact entrance: a venue's postal address and the door you use are not always the same.

Kazimierz is another option for an evening around its bars and cultural venues. Choose a specific destination and check its current programme rather than assuming every venue has live music or the same closing time. Allow travel time between neighbourhoods; the route depends on where you actually start and finish.

Party Do's and Dont's

Getting Around

Save your accommodation address and plan the return journey before leaving. For public transport, use Krakow's transport authority for timetables, maps and ticket information. Check the route for the actual date and time; don't assume your daytime service runs unchanged overnight. If booking a taxi or a ride, confirm the pickup point and fare information with the provider.

Cash or Card?

Check which payment methods the venue accepts before ordering. Confirm that the terminal amount matches your bill, and keep the receipt if you need it. Don't rely on an old guide's exchange rate or assume a venue accepts foreign cash.

Ordering Drinks

Ask for the menu and specify the drink, brand and serving size you want. Check whether a quoted offer has a time limit, an entry charge or other conditions. Our budget-drinks guide explains how to compare prices without relying on old happy-hour promises. Alcohol-free choices can be part of the evening too.

Stay Safe

Keep an eye on your belongings and drinks, agree a meeting point with friends, and ask venue staff for help if something feels wrong. Respect other guests, staff and neighbours. Check any age, ID, dress or entry requirements with the venue; a recommendation or group plan does not override its door policy.
