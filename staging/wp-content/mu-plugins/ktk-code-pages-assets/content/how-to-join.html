HOW TO JOIN THE PUB CRAWL

Choose your date on the regular Krakow pub crawl booking page, check availability and the current price, and complete your booking online. Keep your booking confirmation handy.

The regular crawl starts at 9 PM by Adam Mickiewicz Monument (Adam Statue) in Krakow's Main Market Square. Look for the guides and their pink umbrella. See the meeting-point directions before setting off.

Guests must be 18 or over. Bringing a valid ID is recommended.

Coming back for another night? For a cash return booking, speak with the pub crawl leader to confirm you joined a previous crawl.

For online return bookings, show your previous booking confirmation to the leader to receive a discount code, or use the code shared in the previous day's WhatsApp group.

New Year's Eve has separate groups at 5 PM and 7:30 PM, with meeting points to be announced. Use the NYE event details for that night, rather than the regular meeting instructions.

Read our pub crawl FAQs or contact the local team if you have questions before booking.
