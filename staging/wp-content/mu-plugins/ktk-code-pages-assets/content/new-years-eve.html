The celebrations start at 5:00 PM or 7:30 PM, depending on the group you choose, where we will have not one but TWO HOURS of open service. It includes unlimited drinks. Just ask the staff and they’ll start pouring. During the Power Hour we’ll also be hosting a huge Smorgasbord buffet full of tastiest Polish and International cuisine.

After the double power hour we head on out to party! We’ll be picking our very favourite venues for this night and as it’s New Year’s Eve all of them will be throwing their own special events. With our wristbands you’ll have free entrance and free welcome shots at every venue.
And of course at midnight, we’ll see in the New Year! Beginning 2027 as we mean to go on  partying.
