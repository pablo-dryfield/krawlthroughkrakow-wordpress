The Big Prize Competition

Got a night-out story that still makes your friends laugh? The Big Prize Competition from Krawl Through Krakow is still on. Send us the funniest, most moving or simply most memorable story from your adventures, and let the team enjoy it too.

The competition is active, with no entry deadline currently set. Prize details were confirmed with the KTK team on 15 November 2026. See how to enter below.

How to enter

Use the entry form below to send your name, email address and story. Tell it in your own words: the unexpected friendship, the brilliant coincidence or the moment everyone is still talking about. There is no need to push your limits to have a story worth telling.

Our favourite story wins. The best stories may also appear on the KTK blog, alongside stories from the team. If you would like your story published anonymously, tell us in your message. Avoid including someone else's private details without their permission.

What you can win

  • A EUR50 flight voucher.
  • Three nights in a hostel.
  • Free Pub Crawl access for the winner and a guest for their whole stay.
  • KTK T-shirts.

Have a question about the prize or your entry? Contact the KTK team. We have not set an entry deadline or announced a winner-selection date.
