A hosted hen night with Krawl Through Krakow

Our Krakow Pub Crawl brings a group together for a shared night out. Check its current date, meeting information and inclusions before choosing your booking. The regular crawl is for guests aged 18 and over, and bringing a valid ID is recommended.

If you want private arrangements, use the hen enquiry page. Minimum group sizes, private services, lead times and deposit/cancellation terms are confirmed individually in each quote. Reserved tables, a particular district or specific clubs are not automatic extras on a regular shared ticket.

Start with an Old Town plan

Old Town can bring dinner, bars and dancing into one evening. Pick a meeting point everyone can find and allow time between activities. Use our Krakow nightlife guide to explore the options, then check the venues and event details for your date.

Leave room to explore Kazimierz

Kazimierz is another option for a bar-and-dinner evening. Choose it because the group likes the places you have found, not because an old review promises a fixed tour route. Our Kazimierz bar guide can help you build a shortlist. Check the journey back to your accommodation before the night starts.

Choose a daytime activity that fits the group

Ask everyone what they would enjoy before booking an activity. Compare the actual session, location, duration and group arrangements, and leave enough time for travel and dinner. An idea in this guide is not confirmation that an activity is included in your pub-crawl ticket.

A massage or relaxation session

A massage can be a quieter part of the weekend. Ask the chosen provider about the treatment, appointment length, access needs and group capacity. Tell it about any relevant requirements directly; this guide does not promise a health benefit or a particular treatment as part of a KTK package.

Shooting-range bookings

For this activity, go directly to our current Shooting Range product. Use its booking details and ask about the arrangements for your group rather than relying on the earlier article's firearm, transport or ammunition description. Keep a range activity separate from a drinking session and follow the provider's admission requirements.

Make dancing part of the celebration

A dance class and a club night are different bookings. Decide whether the group wants to learn something together in the afternoon or simply head to a dance floor later. Check the actual event, ticket and venue requirements; a class does not automatically include club admission.

Arranging a group workshop

For a workshop, confirm the instructor, session format, clothing guidance and group size with the provider before paying. If you want KTK to help with a private plan, send us your group enquiry. We will confirm what can be arranged in an individual quote.

Book dinner around the evening

Leave time for a meal rather than squeezing dinner between two distant activities. Pod Aniolami and Morskie Oko publish their own Polish-food menus and booking information. Contact the restaurant about your date, seating and dietary requirements; a mention here is not a table reservation.

Shopping and free time

If shopping is part of the weekend, browse Galeria Krakowska's current directory and opening information. Give the group a clear time and place to meet again. Use the live directory rather than the old article's fixed store count or tenant list.

Find a bar that suits your group

For a beer-led evening, look at House of Beer. For music, see Klub RE's programme. Check the actual menu, event and seating arrangements instead of expecting an old bottle count or guaranteed table. The best choice is one that fits your group, including anyone choosing alcohol-free drinks.

A vodka tasting for the hen group

Our vodka tasting experience has its own booking and current inclusions. View that product for your date rather than assuming the old article's five samples, named brands or a separate pub-crawl route are included. Keep the tasting and any later night out clearly organised.

Questions to settle before booking

Agree the budget, guest count, dates and must-do activities first. Then compare what each booking actually includes and how the group will travel between them. Keep the confirmations together and make sure everyone knows the meeting arrangements.

Is Krakow right for your hen weekend?

Choose Krakow if its mix of food, bars, activities and city time suits your group. It does not need to be called the cheapest or safest destination to be a good choice. Check the full cost of travel, accommodation and the experiences you actually want before deciding.

Bring the plan together

Start with our hen party options for a group enquiry, or book the current Krakow Pub Crawl if a shared night out is what you want. Keep any private extras in the confirmed quote so the organiser and guests know exactly what is booked.
