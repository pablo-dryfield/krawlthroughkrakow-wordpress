Best Pub Crawls in Europe

by DPBowman | Apr 24, 2019 | Party Tips

Malta Pub Crawl and the nightlife in Malta is a popular thing to do for young people. Partying at the clubs in Paceville is often highlight of your adventure. Our Pub Crawl organises games, challenges and free drinks for all our clients. We’re the best pub crawl in town and have been successfully turning strangers into friends for 3 years. Our party team is always ready to party and lead you through the nightclubs. We choose the best clubs in the area such as those cheeky shot bars on the outskirts of paceville or the cool atmosphere of rooftop bars as our main destination. During the Summer the bars & clubs are packed every night of the week. So from Monday to Sunday you can enjoy the beats, beers and people every day! The winter nights are not as busy but you can still hit the clubs at the weekend and even during the week. International student nights are tuesday & thursday at hugo’s passion or Footloose. Friday nights university students gather at Big G’s & Quenchers for pre-drinks. Sunday nights are good for the Maltese 17-21 year olds at Shadow club.

 

You should also try the Boat Party Malta. Party on board the lazy pirate boat party with an open bar in Malta’s blue lagoon. Dance all night to reggaetton tunes and salsa music. The boat party is a top thing to do in Malta and rated on hostel world as the best boat party in europe. Onboard you will get wet, enjoy unlimited drinks, dance like crazy, flirt, swim and basically lose your mind whilst under the careful attention of our experienced team.

 

Bachelor, Stag, hen events are popular on the island of malta, gozo and comino. We organise private events for your stag, bachelor or hen night at the clubs in Malta. Party with us at the best venues for nigtlife on the island. If you’re new to Malta its a great idea to join the pub crawl, it’s a great way to make new friends and hit up the clubs with a cool group of people and avoid the creeps. Malta Pub Crawl chooses its venues according to what’s happening on the night. The meeting point is in Paceville Piazza and the experience includes 30 minutes of free shots, 4 entry drinks, 4 venues and Maltese humour.
