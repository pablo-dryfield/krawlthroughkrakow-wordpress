Krakow Techno Clubs

Krakow’s techno scene is thriving, with numerous clubs offering unforgettable experiences for electronic music enthusiasts. Some of the best venues include Prozak 2.0, Stolarska 5, Klub Poczta Glowna, Add Noce KRK, and Mash Room. Szpitalna 1

Step into the unique blend of art and music at Szpitalna 1. Experience the fusion of art and music at this old tenement house, featuring three distinct rooms each playing a different style of electronic tunes – something to suit everyone’s tastes.

Stolarska 5: Innovative events hosted at an abandoned building

The innovative Stolarska 5 began hosting underground events at an abandoned building before transforming into a full-fledged techno club due to popular demand from locals and tourists alike. With its raw industrial setting and top-notch sound system, it offers a truly authentic rave experience.

Klub Poczta Glowna: Retro vibes showcasing live music and bands

Klub Poczta Glowna,a retro-style venue located in an old post office building, showcases live music acts along with talented DJs spinning some of the best techno tunes around town on weekends.
Check out their Facebook page for upcoming events and performances.

Add Noce KRK: The newest addition to our Krakow techno clubs scene

Add Noce KRK, one of the newest clubs in Krakow, offers a unique blend of techno music with an underground vibe. Boasting a sizable dance area and high-quality audio equipment, Add Noce KRK has quickly become the go-to destination for those looking to experience new music.
Stay updated on their latest events by following them on Facebook.

Mash Room: Located in Plac Wolnica hosting unique techno events

Situated in the heart of Kazimierz district at Plac Wolnica, Mash Room is known for hosting some unforgettable techno parties that cater to both locals and tourists alike. Keep an eye out for their special themed nights and DJ lineups by visiting their Facebook page.

Techno Festivals and Events in Krakow

Krakow’s vibrant techno scene is not limited to clubs alone. Throughout the year, Krakow hosts a plethora of celebrations and gatherings that honor its appreciation for techno, providing unforgettable memories to both its inhabitants and visitors. Two of the most popular events include Roof Party and the Unsound Festival.

Roof Party – Summer Rooftop Extravaganza with Breathtaking Views

Taking place during the summer months, the Roof Party is a unique event that combines stunning views of Krakow with top-notch DJ performances. Hosted on rooftops across the city, partygoers can dance under the stars while enjoying panoramic vistas of this beautiful Polish destination. The atmosphere at these parties is electric as people from all walks of life come together to share their passion for techno music.

Venues:

  • Forum Przestrzenie
  • Strefa Krajowa (National Zone)
  • Pauza In Garden: Located on ulica Rajska (Paradise Street), this venue offers an intimate setting amidst lush greenery.

Unsound Festival – Exploring Experimental Electronic Soundscapes

Held annually in October since its inception in 2003, Unsound Festival has grown into one of Europe’s leading electronic music events. Unsound, which showcases a diverse range of experimental sounds from around the world, Unsound is known for its innovative programming and commitment to pushing boundaries within the genre. Unsound Festival offers an array of activities, including live performances, workshops, panel discussions, and film screenings, to provide a comprehensive exploration of the electronic music scene.

Notable past performers:

  • Ben Frost
  • Aphex Twin
  • Jlin
  • Tim Hecker: Canadian composer renowned for his ambient soundscapes.
