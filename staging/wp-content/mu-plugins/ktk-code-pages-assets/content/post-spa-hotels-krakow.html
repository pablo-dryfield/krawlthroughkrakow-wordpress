A Day at Chocholow Thermal Baths

When in Krakow, Poland, one simply must visit the famous spa resort of Chocholow Thermal Baths. Tucked away amidst the majestic beauty of the Tatra Mountains, this wellness destination is an oasis for those seeking relaxation and rejuvenation.

The thermal waters here are teeming with sulfur, calcium, magnesium, and sodium, minerals that provide a host of health benefits to bathers. So if you’re feeling adventurous or just need some pampering after exploring Krakow’s historical sites like Auschwitz and Wieliczka Salt Mines on your day trips, make sure to pencil in a trip to these baths.
