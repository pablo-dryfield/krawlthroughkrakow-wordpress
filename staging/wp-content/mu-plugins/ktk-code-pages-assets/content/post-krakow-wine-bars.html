Top Wine Bars in Krakow

Uncover the top 4 Krakow wine bars, a must-visit for both casual and experienced drinkers alike when exploring this stunning city. At BARaWINO and other wine bars in Krakow, you can sample a wide variety of wines from around the world, perfect for both casual drinkers and connoisseurs. Enjoy a glass or two at these fantastic venues while exploring this beautiful city.

BARaWINO: A Quiet Haven With Over 500 Wines Available by the Bottle

Located in the heart of the Kazimierz district, BARaWINO is an intimate wine bar that offers more than 500 different bottles to choose from. The knowledgeable staff will help you find your new favourite vintage as you relax in their cosy atmosphere. Don’t forget to try some of their delicious tapas-style dishes, which pair perfectly with your chosen wine.

Czarna’s Stoccaggio Wine Bar: Impressive Global Wine Selection to Suit Every Palate

Czarna’s Stoccaggio Wine Bar, located near Wawel Castle, boasts an extensive range of wines sourced from all corners of the globe. Their friendly sommeliers can guide you through their impressive list and recommend options based on your preferences.

Sababa

In Krakow’s Kazimierz district, there is a fantastic wine bar called Sababa. Upon entering the establishment, you will be met with delightful cocktails, stylish decor, and soothing tunes that create an ideal atmosphere for a memorable evening.

Klimaty Poludnia Hotel & Wine Bar: Quality Original Wines, Delicious Food, and Local Beers

Situated within a beautifully renovated hotel building, Klimaty Poludnia Hotel & Wine Bar offers guests an unforgettable experience with its wide selection of quality wines, delectable dishes made from fresh ingredients, and locally brewed beers. The warm atmosphere invites patrons to relax and savour each sip while enjoying the company of fellow travellers.

For wine aficionados, Krakow wine bars provide an opportunity to sample some of the finest Polish wines in a unique and enjoyable atmosphere.

Exploring Local Wines in Krakow’s Top Venues

In addition to international offerings, some of these exceptional establishments showcase the best of Poland’s local wine scene. Discover the unique flavours and characteristics of Polish wines while enjoying your time in Krakow wine bars.

Wineries Producing Exceptional Quality Bottles

  • Jasiel Winery: Located near Krakow in southern Poland, Jasiel Winery focuses on organic viticulture and produces high-quality reds from their vineyards.
  • Marcin Gortat Vineyard: Owned by famous basketball player Marcin Gortat, this vineyard produces award-winning white wines made from Solaris grapes.
  • Zielona Gora Wine Region: This region boasts several noteworthy wineries, such as Winnica Kapela and Winnica Srebrna Gora, both of which offer excellent-quality wines that showcase the terroir of the area.
