The Ultimate Krakow Weather Guide!

by Jess Campbell | Aug 7, 2019 | Kraków Nightlife, Uncategorized

Choose a Krakow trip for the sights and experiences you want, then pack for the forecast rather than a guaranteed season. A spring weekend, summer night out and winter market visit call for different layers and backup plans.

Guide checked 14 November 2026. The monthly figures below are IMGW's 1991-2020 climate normals for Krakow station 12566: long-term mean daily temperatures averaged over each month, not daytime highs, overnight lows or a forecast. Actual conditions can differ substantially. Use IMGW's current forecast and warnings for your dates.

Krakow in spring: March to May

Plan a spring day with layers you can add or remove, comfortable walking shoes and an indoor stop if conditions change. Do not assume pavement cafes or seasonal attractions will be operating simply because the calendar says spring.

For Easter markets, exhibitions and festivals, check the city's events calendar for the actual year and date. A past programme is not confirmation that an event repeats on the same weekend.

Mix an Old Town walk with a booked visit to Wawel or St Mary's Basilica, checking visitor admission rather than assuming everything is open. Our weekend guide helps group the central sights.

For a Memorial visit, use our Auschwitz visitor guide. For Wieliczka, start with the Salt Mine's official visitor information. Neither visit should be treated as a casual weather-dependent walk without checking bookings and travel.

Krakow weather in March

1991-2020 monthly mean: 3.5 °C. Keep a warmer layer for evenings and check the actual forecast before choosing a long outdoor route.

Krakow weather in April

1991-2020 monthly mean: 9.3 °C. Build a flexible day with a coat or jacket you can carry, plus somewhere indoors if rain interrupts sightseeing.

Krakow weather in May

1991-2020 monthly mean: 14.0 °C. May is not a promise of perfect weather or little rain. Check your dates before committing to an all-outdoor plan.

Krakow in summer: June to August

Give yourself a choice between outdoor sightseeing and an indoor break rather than filling every hour in advance. Check daily temperatures, rain and storm warnings, and plan the walk back as well as the daytime route.

Summer events and terrace opening arrangements vary. Follow the actual operator's programme, not an old description of guaranteed rooftop parties or a fixed sunset time for the whole season.

For a social evening, compare our Krakow Pub Crawl with the music and venue options in our nightlife guide. Check the current inclusions and joining instructions; the regular crawl is for ages 18 and over, and bringing valid ID is recommended.

If you are considering a day at Energylandia, check its calendar and the zones actually operating for your date. A summer water-park description does not establish year-round access, and an old festival link does not confirm this year's programme.

Krakow weather in June

1991-2020 monthly mean: 17.6 °C. Pack for your forecast, including a layer for the journey home and a practical rain option.

Krakow weather in July

1991-2020 monthly mean: 19.3 °C. This is an all-day monthly mean, not the day's maximum. Leave flexibility for hotter or wetter conditions.

Krakow weather in August

1991-2020 monthly mean: 18.9 °C. Check both daytime and evening conditions; a warm afternoon does not decide what you will want after a late night.

Krakow in autumn: September to November

Combine a shorter outdoor route with food, galleries or a booked tasting. If you want a vodka tasting group, use our current Polish Vodka Tasting product for its actual arrangements, inclusions and availability rather than another operator's tour.

Look up dated music listings before booking around a festival. Unsound stages events in different cities; a generic homepage or an old October description is not confirmation of a Krakow event on your weekend.

Rynek Underground is one central indoor option, but it has ticket limits and closure dates to check. Use our practical city guide for arrival, accommodation and food planning rather than relying on old example prices.

Krakow weather in September

1991-2020 monthly mean: 13.9 °C. Keep an evening layer and an indoor alternative instead of assuming summer conditions continue unchanged.

Krakow weather in October

1991-2020 monthly mean: 8.8 °C. Plan sightseeing around the shorter daylight window and your forecast; October is not reliably mild and dry.

Krakow weather in November

1991-2020 monthly mean: 3.8 °C. A warm layer, rain-ready footwear and a flexible route are more useful than assuming a particular amount of snow.

Krakow in winter: December to February

Use our winter activities guide to compare markets, indoor visits, food and seasonal skating. Check the dates of Christmas events and individual venues: decorations, open stalls and snow are not guaranteed throughout winter.

Build in time indoors and check the forecast again before leaving. Krakow city conditions do not describe the Tatra Mountains, and a monthly temperature does not tell you whether a path or activity is suitable.

A Zakopane or mountain day needs its own transport and conditions plan, not a fixed two-hour travel promise. Read TPN's current visitor notice before considering a mountain visit, and ask a qualified guide about suitability rather than treating a city-break article as route advice.

For evenings, use current venue programmes or the Krawl Through Krakow homepage. For 31 December, check our New Year's Eve information separately from the regular crawl; do not assume a past comedy schedule or standard night applies.

Krakow weather in December

1991-2020 monthly mean: -0.5 °C. Plan warm layers and verify holiday opening dates. Neither Christmas snow nor a quiet city is guaranteed.

Krakow weather in January

1991-2020 monthly mean: -1.6 °C. Check the current forecast and footwear needs rather than assuming every day will be snowy.

Krakow weather in February

1991-2020 monthly mean: -0.2 °C. Keep your plans flexible and confirm seasonal attractions before travelling across the city for them.
