How to get from Krakow Airport to the city

Choose your arrival transport around your accommodation, luggage and arrival time. Save the full address before travelling, rather than assuming every service drops you at the Main Market Square.

Train to Krakow Glowny

The airport's published fare to Krakow Glowny is 20 PLN at this check. Follow the terminal signs to the airport station and check the official train information for tickets and the operator's current timetable. Leave time to get from the station to your accommodation; an airport train ticket is not a citywide transport pass.

Public airport buses

The airport currently lists daytime routes 209 and 300 and night route 902. Its published normal single-trip/30-minute ticket is 6 PLN. Check the route, destination, ticket validity and any changes on the airport bus page; do not choose a timed ticket without considering your whole journey.

Airport taxis

For a door-to-door option, use the airport's signed taxi service or a licensed operator you have booked. Confirm the fare and destination before departure. Old example prices are not quotes, and access restrictions may affect the final drop-off point.

Ride-hailing and late arrivals

Check the app's current fare, pickup instructions and vehicle details before getting in. Do not accept an unsolicited ride or assume the cheapest fare is available after a delayed flight. Keep the accommodation's check-in instructions handy and allow for the last part of the journey on foot.

Will I need Polish?

English can help in visitor-facing businesses, but do not assume every person or service can use it. Save addresses in their original Polish spelling, keep your booking confirmation available and use a translation tool when needed.

A few courteous words go a long way: dzien dobry for hello, prosze for please and dziekuje for thank you. Ask about prices and arrangements before ordering rather than relying on a misunderstanding being easy to fix later.

Where Krakow is and how to plan onward travel

Krakow is in southern Poland. Check actual rail or coach departures for journeys to Warsaw, Wroclaw, Prague or Budapest; a journey time from an old article is not a connection you can book.

Use the local date and time on your transport and activity confirmations. Poland changes clocks seasonally, so a fixed GMT offset or a year-round claim about the difference from the UK can mislead. Leave a buffer between a flight, train and a booked activity.

Planning an Auschwitz-Birkenau Memorial visit

Treat this as a separate, carefully planned visit rather than another quick city stop. Read the Memorial's official visitor information and reserve through its official system. Confirm the date, language, visit type and admission arrangements before arranging transport.

Our Memorial visitor guide explains the planning choices. Do not assume you can arrive without a reservation, collect an audio guide or combine the Memorial and the salt mine without checking the actual itinerary.

If travelling independently, check the current transport stop, return service and transfer between the Memorial's sites. If booking an organised visit, read what admission, guiding and pickup are actually included. Allow time for both sites and the journey; this guide does not quote a bus fare or promise a particular travel time.

Choosing accommodation in Krakow

Compare the total stay price, room type and location rather than treating hostels, aparthotels and hotels as a fixed quality ladder. Check late arrival, luggage storage, stairs or lift access, cancellation terms and the journey home after a night out.

Hostels and shared rooms

Look at dorm size, lockers, quiet hours and whether you want a social or quieter property. Compare the options on Greg&Tom's operator site and check the particular property and room, not just the shared brand name. Our hostel guide covers considerate late returns and shared spaces.

Aparthotels and apartments

A kitchenette or separate living space may suit a longer stay, but check the actual unit's facilities and arrival arrangements. For a Main Market Square option, compare the rooms at Venetian House. Confirm the booked room, cleaning arrangements and any extra charges directly; a central address does not mean every room has the same view.

Hotels for a city break

Compare Hotel Copernicus, PURO Krakow Kazimierz and The Bonerowski Palace around your route and preferred setting. Check the current room price, breakfast choice and cancellation terms for the dates you want.

What to budget for Krakow

Build a budget for your own dates: accommodation, airport transfer, city travel, meals, attraction tickets and evenings out. Compare final booking prices in PLN, including luggage, breakfast, deposits or other conditions where relevant. There is no single daily amount that fits a dorm stay, a private hotel room and a celebration weekend.

Our money-saving guide explains useful comparisons, and the free-activities guide separates outdoor exploring from ticketed interiors. Check menus before choosing a table, rather than expecting old beer or meal prices everywhere.

Budgeting for a night out

Set aside money for admission, drinks, food and the trip home. Compare a venue's current terms with the inclusions of an organised night; neither is automatically cheaper for every person. Our budget-drinks guide gives dated examples, not a citywide price promise.

For an organised option, check the current Krawl Through Krakow pub crawl. Compare the inclusions for your selected date with the evening you want, and keep a separate allowance for any extras and the journey home.

Practical precautions in Krakow

Keep valuables secure, check bills and plan your return journey. Do not leave drinks unattended or assume drink spiking only happens in one type of venue. Avoid isolated walks alone at night and use an identifiable, booked vehicle when needed.

The UK government's Poland advice explains these risks. Read our Krakow safety guide for further planning. If something feels wrong, ask venue staff for help and contact the appropriate emergency service when needed.

Weather and packing

Check the IMGW weather service close to your travel dates and pack for the activities you have booked. Layers, comfortable footwear and rain protection are more useful than treating an old seasonal temperature range as a forecast.

Keep an indoor alternative for poor weather and recheck outdoor plans when conditions change. Our weather article offers further background, but snow, clear skies and comfortable outdoor conditions are not guaranteed by the month alone.

Polish food to try

Leave time for meals as well as attractions. Menus vary, so ask about ingredients, portion sizes and dietary requirements before ordering; a dish name is not an allergen or vegetarian guarantee.

Pierogi

Try a savoury or sweet filling, or share a few varieties if that suits your group. Potato-and-cheese, meat, cabbage-and-mushroom and fruit fillings offer different options. Check the preparation and toppings as well as the filling.

Soups, meats and sides

Zurek is a sour rye soup; barszcz is a beetroot soup with different serving styles. Grilled meats, stews and potato dishes are other options to compare on a menu. The Polish Tourism Organisation's food guide introduces regional dishes without making a particular restaurant's ingredients or portions universal.

Zapiekanka and quick bites

A zapiekanka is an open baguette-style snack with toppings; mushrooms and cheese are familiar choices, with variations between vendors. Compare the menu, size and toppings before buying. Street food can be a convenient stop between plans, but check who is actually serving when you visit.

Where to eat

Choose around the part of the city you are visiting rather than crossing town for an unverified opening time. For coffee or breakfast, look at Wesola Cafe; for an Italian meal, check Nolio's current contact and booking information.

Our food guide offers more ideas to compare. Check each venue's current menu, kitchen hours and reservation arrangements, especially for groups, and leave time to eat before a booked evening activity.

Where to go out

Start with the kind of evening you want: a conversation-friendly bar, live music, a particular DJ or an organised social night. Our nightlife hub links to more specific venue guides. Check the actual programme, admission and last transport rather than relying on a fixed old club list.
