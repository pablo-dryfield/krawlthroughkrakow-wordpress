FAQs about Irish Bars Krakow

Why are Irish bars everywhere?

The widespread presence of Irish bars worldwide can be attributed to the large-scale emigration from Ireland during the 19th and 20th centuries. As immigrants settled into new countries, they brought their traditions along with them, including opening pubs that served as gathering spaces for fellow expatriates and others who appreciated their warm ambience and convivial spirit.

What is the oldest Irish bar in the world?

According to Guinness World Records, Sean’s Bar, located in Athlone, Ireland, is the oldest pub in Ireland and across Europe. Established around AD 900 by Luain Mac Luighseach (son of Lewy), this historic establishment continues to serve patrons today while preserving its unique character.
