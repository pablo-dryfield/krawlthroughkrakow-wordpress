Racing Etiquette Unveiled

We’ve all heard the “it’s not whether you win or lose” spiel, right? Well, here  when it comes to go karting Krakow, we mean it. Our unwritten rules prioritize fair play and accident prevention over winning-at-all-costs attitudes.

These include giving way when being overtaken (no road hogs allowed.), respecting flag signals from marshals (yellow means slow down, folks), and keeping hands inside karts unless signaling an issue – think less Fast & Furious, more Driving Miss Daisy.

Remember, though – while there may be rules aplenty, don’t forget our most important commandment: Have Fun.
