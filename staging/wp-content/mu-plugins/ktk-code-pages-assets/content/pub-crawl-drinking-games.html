Drinking Games

Drunk Jenga

Games and download links reviewed 14 November 2026.

KTK Drunken Jenga brings a wobbly tower and a stack of party prompts to the table. Pull a block, read the prompt and keep the tower standing while the rest of the group watches your next move. The taller it gets, the more suspense you have between turns.

Get our KTK Drunken Jenga rules (PDF, 47.8 MB), set up the tower and bring your group together for a game full of conversation and unexpected moments. Find more ideas in our KTK party-game guide.

Kings Cup

A deck of cards, a table and a group of friends are the starting point for King's Cup. Each card brings a new rule, from a quick category round to a rhyme or a change in direction.

Keep the rules where everyone can see them, draw a card in turn and see who can keep up with the changing game. Categories and rhymes are especially good for getting the whole table talking.

Flip Cup

Split into teams and line up your cups on a table. Each player flips a cup from the edge, trying to land it upside down before the next teammate takes over.

For a simple skill round, use empty cups and count successful landings. A stable table and enough space make it easier to follow the action and cheer on your team.

Never have I ever

Start a statement with "Never have I ever..." and discover which friends have a story to tell. Missed trains, surprising travel experiences and kitchen disasters can turn a simple question into a whole conversation.

Keep a few prompts ready so the next person has something to work with. The best moments often come from the story behind an answer.

I’m going on a picnic

Start with "I'm going on a picnic and I'm bringing..." and name an item. The next person repeats the list and adds something new. A perfectly ordinary picnic can quickly turn into a wonderfully strange collection as the round grows.

Try a theme such as food, travel essentials or things beginning with the same letter. This one needs no equipment, just a good memory. For the night out afterwards, see how to join the Krawl Through Krakow pub crawl.
