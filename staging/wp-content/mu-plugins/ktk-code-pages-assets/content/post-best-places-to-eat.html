by DPBowman | Jun 26, 2020 | Krakow Guides

Where to eat during your Krakow trip

Build a day around a proper breakfast, a lunch that fits your route and the evening you want. These are Krawl Through Krakow's food picks, from cafe tables to street-food counters. Compare the current menu and ask about ingredients, rather than choosing only by a photograph or an old bargain price.

Breakfast and cafe stops

Milkbar Tomasza

Milkbar Tomasza remains one of our breakfast recommendations. It is a useful name to consider when your group wants familiar breakfast choices alongside Polish food. Read the day's menu before ordering and allow time to sit down; we do not promise a particular dish, portion or queue length.

Cafe Charlotte

Charlotte at Plac Szczepanski is a bread-and-pastry choice near the Old Town sights. Its branch page lists breakfast options, including bread baskets and croissants. Charlotte also has another Krakow location, so choose the actual branch before checking its menu and table arrangements.

Tamam Cafe & Restaurant

Tamam is our Turkish-breakfast suggestion for a change from a Polish lunch or a quick sandwich. Confirm its current location and breakfast offering before making a special journey. Choose what suits your appetite rather than assuming one photographed spread or an old portion recommendation is today's offer.

Lunch without an outdated price promise

A soup, dumplings or a sit-down main course can fit between sightseeing stops. Compare the complete cost, including extras and drinks, and choose somewhere close to where you actually are. Our milk-bar guide explains another way to approach a Polish lunch.

Gospoda Koko and Bordo

Gospoda Koko and Krakow's Bordo are two of our lunch recommendations. Look at the current board or menu before choosing a set lunch: the dishes, included sides and prices can differ. Check that any website or listing you use belongs to the Krakow venue, not a similarly named restaurant in another city.

Pierogarnia Krakowiacy

For a meal built around pierogi, consider Pierogarnia Krakowiacy. Ask which savoury and sweet fillings are available, and agree whether you want to share a few choices or order individual plates. Check the branch and ingredients rather than assuming every location has the same menu or every filling suits everyone.

Dinner before your evening plans

Am Nam

Am Nam is an Asian-food option in our Old Town list. Compare its current dishes with what your group wants, and leave enough time for a meal before meeting friends or joining an activity. A generous-looking photograph is not a reason to promise that one dish will feed two people.

Warsztat Restaurant & Cafe

Warsztat's own directory distinguishes Restaurant & Cafe around Bozego Ciala/Izaaka from Warsztat Po Polsku at Miodowa 20. Choose the branch and its menu rather than treating the names as one identical offer. If an outdoor table matters, check the arrangement and our weather guide before relying on it.

Okraglak and Plac Nowy

Plac Nowy's central Okraglak is a useful stop for zapiekanki: open-faced toasted baguettes sold by different counters. The city's Plac Nowy page describes the market and its food stands. Compare the actual vendor's toppings and prices; it is not one restaurant with a single menu.

Food trucks and market food

Use our street-food and market guide to distinguish a food-truck stop, a dated food fair and a regular market. Near the station, Galeria Krakowska's restaurant directory is an indoor-dining alternative. Pick the format that fits your time and the weather.

A bar stop with food in mind

442 Sport Pub

442 Sport Pub's operator page lists food such as burgers and fish and chips at Sw. Tomasza 20. If you are coming for a match, confirm the specific screening and whether the kitchen is serving when you intend to eat. A sports-pub recommendation is not a guarantee every event will be shown.

La Bodega del Ron

La Bodega del Ron is one of our choices for the social part of an evening. Check the current kitchen offering before making it your dinner plan, then use our nightlife guide to compare the atmosphere with other venues. For a separate guided tasting, see the Polish vodka experience in our own store.

Late-night food: check the kitchen, not just the bar

Have a food option that fits your route home, and check its actual service hours. A venue being open for drinks does not mean its kitchen is still serving. Our earlier nightlife guide provides more local context; use current operator information for tonight's arrangements.

Przypiecek for pierogi

Przypiecek lists its location at Slawkowska 32 and advertises around-the-clock pierogi service. Use its own menu and contact details for your visit, especially on special dates. Choose the filling you want rather than assuming every flavour mentioned in an older guide is available.

Zapiekanka

Plac Nowy is a natural place to compare zapiekanka counters when it fits your evening route. Check the individual trader's service rather than assuming every shop in the centre is open all night. If you are far away, choose a confirmed nearby food option instead of crossing the city for an unverified opening time.

Sweet stops while exploring

Make room for a bakery or cafe stop between the Old Town, Wawel and Kazimierz. Choose the pastry or dessert you actually want, and ask about ingredients and fillings. Photographs illustrate the food; they are not a menu or an assurance about allergens.

Paczki

Paczki are filled doughnuts, often finished with icing or sugar. Compare the fillings at the bakery you choose; some are fruity, others use different sweet centres. Do not assume all recipes contain the same ingredients, or that a pastry's appearance tells you whether a filling contains alcohol.

Chimney cake

A chimney cake is a spiral-shaped sweet bake that can make a change from a doughnut. Check the stall's actual coatings, fillings and serving options before ordering. A topping or ice-cream version seen in one place is not a promise that every vendor offers it.
