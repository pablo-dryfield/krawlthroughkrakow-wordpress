Best Hotels in Krakow in 2019

by Jess Campbell | Sep 20, 2019 | Krakow Guides

How to use this historical hotel shortlist

This is our original 2019 hotel selection, with planning notes and operator links reviewed on 15 November 2026. The original photographs and publication history remain. The categories below describe that original shortlist, not today's room prices or a new ranking.

Compare the total for your actual dates, room type, cancellation terms and number of guests. Old Booking.com scores and dollar bands are not current quotes. Check breakfast, parking, accessibility, luggage storage and any spa access in the rate you choose rather than assuming they are included.

Old Town, Kazimierz, Podgorze and the area around the main railway station offer different starting points. Choose your base around the places you want to visit and your journey back at night. Our Krakow city guide can help with the wider trip; for an evening together, view the current Krakow Pub Crawl separately from your accommodation.

Original budget-friendly shortlist

These four properties appeared in our original lower-budget selection. Their present rates can vary with dates and demand; compare like-for-like rooms and the full stay cost before deciding.

Art & Garden Residence

Art & Garden Residence identifies its address as Krowoderska 71. Its garden and restaurant setting made it part of our original shortlist for visitors looking beyond the busiest streets of the Old Town.

Check the room photographs and access arrangements for the room you select, then compare the walk or transport connection with your plans. Breakfast and parking should be checked in the actual offer, not inferred from the old review.

Lwowska 1

Lwowska 1 Aparthotel is at Lwowska 1/14 in Podgorze. The operator offers rooms and apartments, with kitchenettes in some categories, plus a restaurant and a separate wellness offering.

This is a different base from staying beside the Main Market Square. Compare the specific apartment layout and your routes across the river; ask about any equipment or wellness access that matters to your stay rather than assuming every room has it.

Hotel Jan

Hotel Jan is at Grodzka 11 in the Old Town. Its operator describes a historic townhouse with a conservatory breakfast room, giving visitors a central starting point for exploring on foot.

Compare the room size and bed arrangement for your group and check whether breakfast is included in your chosen rate. If you are arriving by car or need a particular access arrangement, confirm it before booking a room in the historic centre.

Hotel Eden

Hotel Eden identifies Ciemna 15 in Kazimierz as its address. Its connected historic townhouses put this original shortlist entry in a different neighbourhood from the hotels around the Main Market Square.

Read the property's current arrival instructions, particularly for a late arrival, rather than relying on old reception-hour claims. Compare the selected room and your evening return journey with the rest of your Krakow plans.

Original mid-range shortlist

The original selection mixed boutique hotels and apartment-style stays. The useful comparison today is the room, location and booking terms you need, not the old dollar symbols or an undated review score.

Queen Boutique Hotel

Queen Boutique Hotel is at Dietla 60, between the Old Town and Kazimierz areas. Its operator presents the Amarylis restaurant and Queen Beauty & SPA alongside its rooms.

Use the current room and spa information to plan your stay; treatments or private sauna arrangements are not automatically part of every room rate. Check the total offer and any reservation needed for a meal or spa visit.

Metropolitan Boutique Hotel

Metropolitan Boutique Hotel is at Berka Joselewicza 19. Its operator describes a renovated historic building between Old Town and Kazimierz, with rooms, a restaurant and a gym.

It remains a useful location to compare if your plans cross those two areas. Check the actual room category, breakfast and any current restaurant or music programme directly; a past programme or special offer is not a promise for your dates.

Use the current property name, room descriptions and booking terms when comparing a stay. The photographs below remain from the original article and should not be treated as confirmation of today's room layout or included services.

Hotel Polski Pod Bialym Orlem

The Hotel Polski operator identifies Pijarska 17, near the Old Town's northern edge. Its current site provides room information and details of the Pijarska 17 restaurant.

Compare your selected room and arrival arrangements rather than transferring the old score or price band to a new booking. If parking, a particular room configuration or a group meal matters, confirm those details with the hotel.

Hotel Wawel

Hotel Wawel identifies Poselska 22 as its address. Despite its name, use that actual Old Town address for directions rather than assuming accommodation inside the castle.

Check the current room and arrival information, especially if you have luggage, access requirements or a car. Compare the included services in the rate you choose; the original photographs are a historical illustration, not a room allocation.

Hotel Nobilton: current details unconfirmed

Our original shortlist placed Hotel Nobilton at Pilsudskiego 25. We have not verified its current services directly with the operator, so the old breakfast, restaurant, room and price claims are not repeated as current facts.

Confirm the property's current identity, booking availability and arrival details before paying. An inaccessible website alone does not establish that a hotel is closed; equally, an old recommendation does not confirm today's offer.

Aparthotel Stare Miasto

Aparthotel Stare Miasto identifies Golebia 2 in the Old Town. The operator describes apartments with kitchen facilities, making the exact apartment layout a useful part of the comparison.

Check the room's equipment and sleeping arrangement rather than assuming all apartments are the same. For a group or a longer stay, compare the full cost and contact the property about any particular appliance, access need or arrival arrangement.

Use the current property's room information and terms when booking. We do not carry over the original soundproofing, breakfast or kitchen-equipment claims, or attach a date to a name change that has not been independently established.
