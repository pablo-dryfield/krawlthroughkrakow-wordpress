Read our most Frequently Asked Questions

Welcome to the FAQ page of Krawl Through Krakow, your ultimate guide to exploring Krakow nightlife with our professional guides.

Our mission is to ensure that every traveler gets the most out of their visit, which is why we’ve compiled a comprehensive list of frequently asked questions to assist you in planning your adventure. We’re here to help you experience Krakow like never before. Let’s party together!

How much does the crawl cost?

120 zl per person and there’s a discount for returning customers so keep hold of your wristbands

How can I join?

To join just drop us a quick message through the contact form and we’ll automatically guarantee you a spot. If you’re also interested in one of the other activities we offer just mention it and we’ll get straight back to you.

Do you guys run every night?

Yes we’re there for PubCrawl every single night of the year. If there’s people up for partying we’ll be running!

Where is your meeting point

We meet every day at the Adam Mickiewicz statue in the main market square at 9pm. We have a big pink pub crawl sign so it’s easy to spot us. You can find a map below our contact form on the booking page and front page.

What if I'm late?

If you’re going to be late drop us a message and we’ll let you know where the starting bar is. It’s probably better to message us through Facebook in this instance as it’s harder to check the emails once we’re out partying.

What bars/clubs are we going?

The Pub Crawl co-operates with well over 20 bars and clubs in town. We can’t usually say which one’s we’ll be visiting on a certain night as we let out party guides choose the destinations on the evenings. This really separates us from other Pub Crawls as most don’t give their guides this freedom. Our way the guides can pick the best possible venues each night based on the type of crowd and their amazing knowledge of Krakow’s events and nightlife.

How long is the Pub Crawl?

We start at 9 and usually get to the final club around 1am. Some of the clubs in Krakow however stay open until 11am the next day and some of the bars simply don’t close. So in reality the party lasts as long as the guests want!

Do you stay close to the main square?

Yes. There’s over 900 alcohol licenses in the historic Old Town alone which means we never have to go far and there’s little chance of getting lost and ending up in the middle of nowhere whilst with us.

What if I'm Alone?

The pub crawl is a great activity if you are a solo traveller, The majority of our guides have been or are currently solo travelling so they know what it is like to be a solo traveller and do everything they can to bring the whole group together, The one hour of free drinks along with some drinking games is also the perfect way to get everyone in the group mingling and weather you come as a solo traveller or with a group of friends you are sure to leave the night with a tonne of new friends and some great memories!

Is there a Dress Code?

There’s no dress code for the pubcrawl you can come in whatever you are comfortable in, some of our guests like to dress up while some of our guests prefer to be casual. A good guide is to wear what you would wear on a night out at home. There are some limitations sadly with fancy dress or costumes as some clubs we visit wont allow this, We would recommend in this case bringing a spare set of clothes or some clothes underneath the costume in case you are asked to remove it.

Where are your Photos?

The photos are uploaded to our Facebook page here : https://www.facebook.com/krawlthroughkrakow/photos. They usually take a few days after the night to edit & upload so check back after 4-5 days to see the photos from the night you joined. You can also check out our Instagram page if you are looking for photos to check out what a night on the pub crawl looks like : https://www.instagram.com/krawlthroughkrakow/
