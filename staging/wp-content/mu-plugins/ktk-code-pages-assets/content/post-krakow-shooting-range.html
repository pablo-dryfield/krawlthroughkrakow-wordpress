Safety Measures & Requirements

Before you channel your inner John Wick, it’s essential to keep safety in mind. Unless an adult guardian is present throughout the session, all participants must be 18 years of age or older. And don’t even think about showing up tipsy; intoxicated individuals may be refused service at the discretion of tour providers. After all, booze and bullets don’t mix.

Your well-being is a top priority at these ranges, so they provide essential safety equipment such as ear and eye protection during live-fire sessions. Just remember: always follow your instructor’s guidance and never point a firearm at anything you’re not willing to destroy.
