Dive Bars in Krakow

For those looking for a more down-to-earth drinking experience in Krakow, dive bars offer an unbeatable combination of affordability and local flavour. Often patronized by locals who appreciate affordable booze without any pretense regarding decor or service, these spots offer an authentic taste of Polish nightlife. In this section, we will introduce you to some notable dive bars, including Baniluka, Pub Propaganda, and Free Pub.

Baniluka: Popular among sports enthusiasts with big screens for games

Baniluka is a favourite spot for sports enthusiasts looking for a cheap beer while watching their favourite teams on big screens. With its laid-back atmosphere and friendly staff, Baniluka is perfect for those wanting to enjoy a night out without breaking the bank.

Pub Propaganda: A grungy spot perfect for budget-friendly drinks

If you’re into gritty atmospheres with wallet-friendly prices, look no further than Pub Propaganda. This grungy bar offers an extensive range of drinks at reasonable prices that won’t leave your pockets empty after a fun-filled night.

Free Pub: Another option for cheap drinks while mingling with fellow travellers

Last but not least, Free Pub offers another great spot for budget-conscious drinkers looking to mingle with fellow travellers. Located in the heart of Krakow’s Old Town, this lively establishment serves up reasonably priced beverages alongside fun events such as pub quizzes and karaoke nights.

In short, when it comes to finding where’s the cheap places to drink in Krakow without sacrificing quality or atmosphere, these dive bars have got you covered. Why not take advantage of the great atmosphere and prices these dive bars offer on your next visit?

Krakow has some great dive bars that offer affordable drinks and a unique local experience. Baniluka, Pub Propaganda, and Free Pub are some notable options to check out for budget-friendly drinking while mingling with locals or fellow travellers.

Historic Bar Mleczny Establishments

If you’re looking for where’s the cheap places to drink in krakow, look no further than Krakow’s historic bar Mleczny establishments. These bars serve up cheap beer, wine, cocktails, coffee, soft drinks, and shots in an authentic setting that’ll transport you back in time.

The Origins of Bar Mleczny

A bar Mleczny, or “milk bar,” is a Polish cafeteria-style eatery that dates back to the communist era. Originally subsidized by the government to provide affordable meals for workers, these establishments have evolved into popular spots for budget-conscious travellers seeking delicious food and drink options.

  • Bar Mleczny Today: While many milk bars still offer traditional Polish dishes at low prices (think pierogi and placki ziemniaczane), they’ve also expanded their menus to include inexpensive alcoholic beverages like beer, wine, and vodka shots – a great place for those who want a night out without breaking the bank.
  • An Authentic Experience: Visiting a bar Mleczny is not only about enjoying cheap drinks; it’s also an opportunity to immerse yourself in Poland’s rich history and cultural heritage. The simple interiors with wooden benches harken back to simpler times when Poles gathered together over hearty meals after long days at work.

So, why not step back in time and experience a piece of Poland’s history while enjoying some budget-friendly beverages? With the charm of Krakow’s historic bar Mleczny establishments, you’ll be sure to have an unforgettable night out.

Where can I find the cheapest beer in Krakow?

You can find some of the cheapest beers at Pijalnia Wodki i Piwa chain locations throughout the city. They offer local beer brands at very low prices. Other options include Komisarat Bar and Alchemia Singer Bar, both located within the Jewish Quarter.

Are there any student discounts available on drinks in Krakow?

In general, students may receive discounted drink prices by showing their valid student ID cards at certain establishments. Some popular venues that cater to students include Cosmic Minigolf Pub and Bull Pub, which often have promotions targeting younger crowds.
