Where’s the Cheap Places to Drink in Krakow: Insider Tips

by DPBowman | Jul 11, 2023 | Uncategorized

For cheap drinks in Krakow, start with a menu you can actually compare: the named drink, serving size, branch and any entry charge matter more than an old claim about the city's cheapest bar. Our examples below come from the venues' own published menus, not a citywide price survey.

Price check by Krawl Through Krakow: 13 November 2026. The menus do not display an effective date. Treat these as examples shown when checked, and confirm the current menu at the venue before ordering.

Places to start: compare their own menus

Banialuka, Chicas & Gorillas and Oldsmobil are on the Krawl Through Krakow team's recommended-venue list. They offer different kinds of evenings; naming them here does not mean every drink is the cheapest in town or that they are stops on a fixed pub-crawl route.

Banialuka: beer, cocktails and alcohol-free options

The Banialuka website lists branches at Szewska 13, plac Szczepański 6 and Floriańska 3. Its published menu displays these examples:

  • Tyskie draught beer: 400 ml, 11 PLN.
  • Aperol Spritz: 290 ml, 25 PLN.
  • Listed zero-alcohol cocktails: 290 ml, 25 PLN.
  • Mineral water: 500 ml, 7 PLN.

Check the menu in your chosen branch, including the actual drink and serving. This is Banialuka's menu, not a price list for the separate Pijalnia Wódki i Piwa chain.

Oldsmobil: a different cocktail comparison

Oldsmobil's contact page gives its address as Św. Tomasza 31. The cocktail panel linked from its own menu page shows an Aperol Spritz at 210 ml for 36 PLN and a Blue Lagoon at 250 ml for 34 PLN.

Notice the different stated serving sizes. The same cocktail name does not prove identical recipes, ingredients, alcohol content or value. Use the examples to decide what suits your evening, not to assume every item at one bar costs less than every item at another.

Chicas & Gorillas: choose the branch first

The operator's locations page lists Krakow branches in Pasaż Bielaka and at plac Szczepański 5, with menu links. Check the Krakow menu for your branch; a different city's menu or an old promotion is not a current Krakow quote.

Compare more than the headline price

  • Name and size: compare the actual beer, cocktail or alcohol-free drink and the stated serving.
  • Conditions: check the date, time window, qualifying products and any entry charge before relying on a promotion.
  • The whole evening: allow for food, transport, cloakroom or entry costs where applicable, not just one drink.
  • At the bar: specify what you want, confirm the price and check the bill. Buying more than you want is not a saving.

Check current opening times separately. An old guide's late closing time does not confirm that a branch is open on your date.

Plan an area, not an assumed price bracket

Choose a few venues near one another instead of crossing the city for an unconfirmed deal. The Old Town and Kazimierz both offer places to consider, but an address alone does not establish a cheap menu. See our Krakow nightlife overview for the wider team shortlist and planning advice.

Earlier recommendations included Pub Propaganda, Free Pub, Ulica Krokodyli and Duffy's Irish Bar. Their earlier descriptions are not current price checks in this edition; confirm the venue's own menu before relying on an old offer.

If you want sports, live music or a particular atmosphere, check that programme as well as the price. A cheap-drinks list does not confirm what will be showing or playing that night.

Keep food in the budget too

Choose somewhere to eat as a separate part of the plan, and check its menu and opening time. A recommendation for an inexpensive meal is not evidence that the same place serves beer, cocktails or late-night drinks.

Budget-drinks questions

Are there any happy-hour specials?

Use the venue's current announcement or menu and check the exact day, hours, branch and conditions. We are not promising a fixed daily happy hour or free club admission in this guide.

Where can I find the cheapest beer in Krakow?

We have not checked every bar, so we cannot honestly name the city's cheapest. One published example is Banialuka's 400 ml Tyskie at 11 PLN when checked on 13 November 2026. Compare the same serving and current local menu rather than an old citywide claim.

Are student discounts available?

Do not assume a student discount applies. Ask the venue about its current offer, any accepted student ID, the day and the products covered before ordering.

Choose the evening that suits you

If you prefer a group plan, compare the current Krakow pub-crawl inclusions and available dates with the entry, drinks and travel you would arrange yourself. Check what is actually included rather than assuming a tour covers every venue or every drink you might want.
