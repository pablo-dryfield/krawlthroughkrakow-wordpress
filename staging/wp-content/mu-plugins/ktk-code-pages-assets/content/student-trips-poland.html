Organising a Krakow trip for a university society, sports team or group of friends? Start with your dates, headcount and budget, then use the student-trip enquiry form to explain what your group wants to do.

Give your organiser a useful starting point

  • Who is travelling: tell us the group size, age range and whether numbers are provisional. Include your society or university and one contact person.
  • Your timetable: share arrival and departure dates, fixed commitments and the time you want available for sightseeing, meals and group activities.
  • Your budget and priorities: give a budget per person and explain what it should cover. Ask which costs, transport and extras are included in any proposal.
  • Before confirming: check availability, minimum numbers, private or shared arrangements, payment deadlines, cancellation terms and the process for changing the headcount.

The regular Krakow pub crawl is an evening option for guests aged 18 and over; bringing valid ID is recommended. It starts at 9 PM and is a shared night with other travellers, not an automatically private student event. NYE has separate arrangements.

For other plans, compare the current tour options. An enquiry does not reserve places or guarantee a student discount; confirm the selected offer and its terms before collecting money from the group.
