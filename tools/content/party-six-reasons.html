1. An organised evening

A crawl can be useful when you want a plan rather than choosing every venue independently. Start with the current joining information and decide whether the offer fits your preferences. An organiser cannot guarantee that a venue, group or type of music will suit every guest.

2. Clear joining information

Use the instructions for your own booking before travelling. An old photograph, article or meeting-time mention is not a replacement for current arrangements. Check the FAQs and contact the team if something important is unclear; particular group requests are confirmed individually.

3. A team to ask

You can ask the KTK team about joining and the arrangements for your evening. Discuss practical needs in advance rather than assuming every plan or venue is suitable. A useful answer explains what is arranged and what still needs checking; it does not promise an outcome for every guest.

4. Know what your booking includes

The regular crawl's reviewed offer includes {{openBarHours}} hour of open bar. Check the current product and booking conditions for exactly what is included, instead of relying on an old list of drinks or prices. An inclusion is an option, not a requirement to drink or a challenge to use as much as possible.

You can choose an alcohol-free option, decline an activity or finish early. For practical preparation and official information, see the Krakow safety guide. No booking removes every risk.

5. A social setting

An organised night offers a setting in which to meet other guests, but the people attending and the size of the group can vary. You do not need to match anybody else's drinking, spending or participation to join in a conversation. Respect another person's choice to decline, take a break or keep their plans private.

6. Understand entry arrangements

Check what your booking says about venue entry and follow the actual venue conditions. The old promise of no queues or an unchanged route should not be treated as a guarantee. Clothes, identification, capacity and other venue arrangements can affect entry; ask the team about your circumstances instead of assuming a group booking overrides them.

KTK pub crawls are for adults aged {{minimumAge}} and over; bringing valid ID is recommended. Read how to join and your own booking confirmation for the current offer. For a different style of night, compare the nightlife guide and make the arrangements that work for you.
