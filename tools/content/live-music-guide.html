Choose the right kind of music evening

  • A jazz-led performance: compare the current Harris, PIEC Art and U Muniaka programmes.
  • Dinner with music: check The Piano Rouge's dining-room and performance arrangements.
  • Cabaret, recital or a listed concert: start with Piwnica pod Baranami's programme, including the performance language.
  • A restaurant with seasonal events: check Hard Rock Cafe's actual calendar, not an assumed nightly jazz schedule.
  • A club or concert event: verify the specific Forty Kleparz listing rather than treating every bar reservation as a concert booking.

Hard Rock Cafe: restaurant and seasonal events

Hard Rock Cafe Krakow describes a restaurant on the Main Market Square with seasonal regular and one-off performances, DJ sets and special events. It is not being presented here as a specialist jazz club. Check the operator's event calendar for what is actually scheduled and confirm any event and table arrangements separately.

Harris Piano Jazz Bar: choose the performance

Harris Piano Jazz Bar gives its address as Rynek Glowny 28 and describes a programme covering jazz and other styles. Use current event information or contact the venue for your date. An old announcement or a general description of the bar does not confirm that your preferred artist is playing.

PIEC Art: concert room or bar evening?

PIEC Art distinguishes its cocktail bar from cellar concert and bar spaces. Check the dated event, room and ticket arrangements. A drink at the bar and a seated concert can require different plans; confirm what you are reserving.

Cabaret, recital and dinner with music

Not every performance venue is a jazz club, and not every music evening follows a concert format. Read the actual programme: cabaret, a vocal recital and restaurant background music can be quite different experiences. Check the language and whether you want a meal, a performance or both.

Piwnica pod Baranami: read the programme

Piwnica pod Baranami presents a cabaret and performance programme, including recitals and listed concerts. Use its programme page to select an event, then check the full date, year, performance language and ticket information. It is not a promise that every visit is a jazz concert.

The Piano Rouge: confirm the dining room and music

The Piano Rouge identifies a restaurant at Main Market Square 46 and describes live smooth jazz in its concert dining room. Confirm the performance and your room or table when reserving. Do not assume a particular artist, on-request repertoire or separate event admission from a general restaurant description.

Jazz tickets and club events

U Muniaka: admission is not the same as a seat

Jazz Club u Muniaka publishes its programme at Florianska 3. Check the specific performance and the reservation terms: a ticket reservation and a seated-place reservation are different. Confirm the arrangement that matters to your group rather than assuming every entry reservation includes a table.

Forty Kleparz: check the event and its year

Forty Kleparz's music-club page identifies Kamienna 2 and describes concerts and DJ events, alongside its wider restobar offering. Some site material is historical and calendar entries may not show a clear year. Confirm a current dated event with the operator, including whether you need an event ticket, a venue reservation or both. We are not promising a nightly programme or a particular room layout.

Before you book

  • Event: confirm the artist or format, date, year, start time and actual venue.
  • Booking: check admission, seating and table arrangements; a meal reservation is not automatically a concert ticket.
  • Practical needs: ask about language, access and group seating before committing.
  • The evening: allow time for food and travel rather than relying on an old guide's universal walking time.
