The best pub crawl for your trip is one that fits your city, dates and idea of a good night. A hosted group can be a fun way to meet people and discover places together, but two tours with the same name can have very different arrangements. Start with the actual operator and current booking information.

Choose the night, not just the headline

Think about whether you want conversation, games, dancing or a particular music night. Compare the meeting arrangements and how the group moves between venues. A large drinks allowance or a long list of stops does not tell you whether the experience suits you. Check what the ticket actually covers and what you would pay for separately.

This article's earlier Malta description included specific weeknight recommendations, meeting details and drink counts. Do not use those old details as a current itinerary. Ask the operator if something matters to your group, including venue admission, accessibility or alcohol-free choices.

Boat parties and private events are separate choices

If you are interested in a boat party, stag event or hen celebration, start from the operator's current experience links and ask what is actually available on your date. A photograph or an old article link does not establish a live departure, private arrangement, transport service or included activity. Keep the supplier and cancellation terms clear before you pay.

Compare the complete booking

  • Confirm the date, start time, meeting place and age/ID requirements.
  • Check admission conditions and exactly which drinks, entries or activities the ticket includes.
  • Plan the journey to the meeting point and back to your accommodation.
  • Ask about access needs and group arrangements before booking, rather than assuming every venue works the same way.
  • Read the operator's current change/cancellation terms and keep your confirmation.
