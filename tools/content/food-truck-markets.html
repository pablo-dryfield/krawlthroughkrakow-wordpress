Stary Kleparz: a market and selected food events

Stary Kleparz's operator describes a regular produce market with food businesses and Art & Food Bazar on selected Sundays. Treat the bazaar as an announced event, not a permanent grassy food-truck park with picnic facilities.

Check the edition and traders

The city's market-events programme lists individual dates and changes. At this review, its 15 November 2026 Art & Food Bazar entry is cancelled. Recheck your intended edition and what is actually trading, rather than making a special trip based only on a recurring-event description.

Plan for the actual space

Before meeting a group, check shelter, seating and the route through the market. Do not assume a produce-market visit includes a lawn, picnic tables or room for everyone to sit together. Keep walkways clear and respect the people shopping and working there.

Compare the current offer

Plac Nowy and the Okraglak counters

The city's Plac Nowy information describes the market, central Okraglak and food stands, including zapiekanki. Choose an individual counter and compare its toppings and price. Plac Nowy is not one restaurant or one shared menu.

Choose a counter, then check where to eat

Look for the actual trader and a suitable place to enjoy the food. Do not assume a street-food stop includes a particular two-storey patio, reserved seating or a bar package. If you need a sit-down meal, compare the nearby options in our food guide.

Street-food choices around Kazimierz

Use actual business names and addresses when arranging to meet in Kazimierz. The neighbourhood's synagogues, courtyards, bars and food traders are separate places; do not treat a religious landmark as the name or entrance of a food business.

Keep the neighbourhood in mind

Allow time to explore without blocking entrances or leaving packaging behind. Keep noise down near homes and places of worship. A street-food photograph is not a seating plan or evidence that a specific courtyard is open for public dining.

Pick the food you want to try

Look for the current counter's offer: a toasted zapiekanka, pierogi with different fillings or another snack that suits your group. Ask about ingredients and portion sizes. Do not assume every market sells all of these dishes or that all traders operate the same hours.
