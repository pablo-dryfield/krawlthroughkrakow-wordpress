Dinner and adult entertainment: check the actual booking

A dinner reservation, show and club-entry package can cover very different things. Agree the type of evening with the group first, then confirm the venue, meal, entry and total cost directly. Do not assume a generic club listing includes a meal, particular performers, table service or a private show.

Choose a format the group actually wants

A shared dinner, music night or club visit can all give the evening a focus. Check the programme and what's reserved rather than treating adult entertainment as compulsory for a stag celebration. Respect performers, venue staff and other guests.

Questions to ask before paying

  • What does the quoted price include, and what is charged separately?
  • What are the entry, age, clothing and photography conditions?
  • How are dietary needs, group-size changes and cancellation handled?

Krakow stag-planning questions

Answer the practical questions before committing the group's money. A shorter confirmed plan is more useful than a full timetable built on assumed inclusions.

What can a stag night include?

A meal, a shared activity and a night out make an easy starting point. Compare the groom's preferences, your budget and the actual dates. You can combine a daytime booking with a pub crawl, but leave realistic travel and meal time between them.

Does a stag weekend need a set formula?

No fixed list of activities makes a celebration more authentic. Pick plans the groom and group will enjoy rather than copying a stereotype. Shared stories, a competition or a good meal can be just as central as the club night.

Who pays for the groom?

Agree within the group whether everyone contributes towards the groom's costs. Do not assume a free groom place: any group offer or discount must be confirmed in your actual quote or booking. Make sure everyone understands the amount and what's included before collecting payment.
