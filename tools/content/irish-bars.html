Music and sports: ask for the actual programme

For a match, ask whether your fixture and competition will be shown, whether the commentary has sound and whether you can reserve a view of the screen. For music, check the performer, date, start time and any admission charge. A pub can suit both kinds of evening without offering both at once.

If the performance matters more than the pub format, compare our live-music guide and jazz guide. They distinguish concert tickets, restaurant tables and informal bar visits.

Going with a group or on your own

A shared match or music night gives you something to talk about, but nobody owes a conversation. For a larger group, contact the pub with your date, approximate numbers and whether you want food or a seated event. Agree a meeting point and a return plan before splitting up.

Check the drink and food you actually want

If Guinness or a particular whiskey is the reason for your visit, check the current menu and availability. The archive photograph above illustrates the pub format, not today's stock at a named venue. Compare serving sizes and prices before ordering, and ask about food service separately from the bar's opening time. Non-alcoholic drinks are a valid choice; ask what is available.

Keep the practical parts simple

Watch your drink, check the bill and arrange your journey home. Official Poland travel advice explains the relevant precautions; no recommended venue can guarantee a risk-free evening. Use current public-transport information rather than a fixed route or fare copied from an old guide.

Common questions

Does an Irish-pub name guarantee Irish music?

No. The name describes the pub's identity, not a timetable. Check the advertised performance or match, especially if it is the main reason for choosing the venue.

Should a pub's age decide where to go?

A historical claim does not answer whether you can get a table, watch your match or eat at the time you arrive. This Krakow guide prioritises those practical decisions rather than ranking world-record claims that do not help plan your visit.
