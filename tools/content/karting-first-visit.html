What to expect from a first session

Rental karting puts you on a circuit in a small vehicle for a timed session or an organised race format. Your visit also needs time for check-in, equipment and a briefing. Ask how sessions are grouped if you want to drive with friends rather than assuming every booking means a private race.

Read the rules before you go

Karting is not risk-free. Read the chosen venue's current rules and listen to its staff. Motodrom's rules and GoKarting Center's rules are operator sources, not universal instructions for every track. If you have a suitability question, discuss it before committing to a session.

Equipment, clothing and fit

Ask what the ticket includes and what you need to bring. GoKarting Center's first-visit information covers arrival, clothing and helmet liners. Check sizes and any additional equipment charges with your chosen venue; a photograph or a different operator's package cannot confirm them.

Driver requirements are specific to the venue and kart

Do not assume a single age limit applies to every Krakow track. Ask about height, vehicle fit, supervision and any documentation for each driver. GoKarting Center publishes different vehicle categories; confirm the applicable requirements directly rather than relying on one number from an older guide.

Karting and the regular pub crawl also have different admission rules. The crawl is for ages {{minimumAge}} and over, and bringing valid ID is recommended. A parental consent form does not make an underage guest eligible for the pub crawl.

Keep racing and drinking separate

Follow the track's briefing and signals rather than treating other drivers as obstacles. GoKarting Center's published rules prohibit participation after alcohol or substances affecting reactions. Plan karting before drinks, and check the staff's instructions if you need help during a session.

Check the kart actually offered

Vehicle fit and the session rules matter more than a generic list of engines. Ask which kart and category your booking uses, whether your group will be split between sessions, and how results are recorded. Availability for a particular vehicle must be confirmed by the operator.

Do not assume a choice of electric or petrol

Rental fleets vary. GoKarting Center's current vehicle page describes LPG-powered karts, so an electric-versus-petrol comparison does not establish what you can book there. Avoid general claims that every electric kart is faster, silent or impact-free; choose from the actual fleet and format the venue confirms.

Your first-visit checklist

  • Confirm the branch, date, arrival time and whether the booking is a shared session or a private arrangement.
  • Check every driver's eligibility, footwear and equipment needs before travelling.
  • Allow for the briefing, breaks and return journey, not just the advertised minutes behind the wheel.
