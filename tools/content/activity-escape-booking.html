Choosing an escape room for a Krakow stag do

Start with the number of people who actually want to play. A large stag group may need separate rooms or start times, rather than squeezing everyone into one team. Ask whether the briefing, clues and hints are available in English and whether the operator can accommodate your whole group at the time you want.

What is an escape room?

An escape room is a timed, story-led puzzle game. Your group searches for clues and connects information to complete the mission. The selected scenario determines the time, capacity and style; do not assume every room lasts an hour or involves being physically locked in. Ask the operator how to leave or pause the game if you need to.

Choose the theme before the difficulty

Compare detective, adventure, fantasy and horror themes with the groom's interests. A mixed-experience group may enjoy a clear shared story more than a room chosen only for its difficulty score. Ask about actors, darkness, confined spaces, physical tasks and step-free access where these affect your choice. A promotional image alone does not answer those questions.

Fear Factory: a horror attraction

Fear Factory presents a house-of-fear experience built around darkness and a pursuing character. Consider it for a group that wants a horror attraction, rather than assuming it is a conventional puzzle room. Check the selected show's conditions and intensity with the operator.

ExitRoom: compare its current puzzle rooms

ExitRoom's own visitor page describes a small-team, one-hour puzzle format. That is this operator's description, not a duration or capacity rule for every Krakow escape room. Confirm the scenario, group arrangement and language before reserving.

LockedUP and Gamescape: check the selected scenario

LockedUP remains a venue to check directly for its current rooms and availability. Gamescape lists several themes with differing durations and capacities, alongside separate group enquiries. Compare the room itself instead of treating every Gamescape option as horror. We have not confirmed the old Exit Games Bunker description, so do not use that story to plan a current booking.

Cost and booking information

Compare the complete price for your actual group and date. Check whether the quote covers one room, one team or each participant, and whether your numbers require a second session. A cheap-looking per-person figure can be misleading if it assumes a full team.

Ask for the total, not an old average

Use the operator's current PLN price and cancellation conditions rather than an old dollar estimate. Before paying, confirm the room, date, start time, team size, language and arrival deadline. Do not assume a stag discount, free groom place or special promotion unless it is included in your confirmation.

Leave room in the day for food and travel

Allow time for the briefing and the journey between activities, not just the advertised game length. Agree where everyone will meet afterwards if your group splits into teams. Keep the puzzle session separate from drinking plans and follow the selected venue's participation conditions.
