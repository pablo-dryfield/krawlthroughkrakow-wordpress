A club night and a festival need different plans

A club ticket usually concerns a particular night; a festival can involve several venues, separate sessions and different admission products. Read what the actual ticket covers before arranging travel or combining events.

Roof parties: check the seasonal event

KTK recommends looking at the Roof Party programme. Check the exact date, venue, weather policy and ticket terms. A rooftop photograph or a past summer listing does not establish a winter opening or access to a particular building.

Unsound: use the correct city and edition

The 2026 Warsaw/Krakow edition is now a past event. Its ticket terms distinguish passes and separately ticketed events. Use the organiser's current edition information for a future visit; no next-edition dates are confirmed in this guide.

Choose by artist and sound, not a residency assumption

Listen to a recent set or release before choosing a night. A DJ set, a live set and a back-to-back performance can be different experiences. A name mentioned in a city guide is not confirmation that the artist will be at a local club during your trip.
