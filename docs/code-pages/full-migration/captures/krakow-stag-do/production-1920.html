Stag Do Krakow Events

Embark on a legendary stag do in Krakow with a mix of adrenaline-packed activities, cultural excursions, and unforgettable nightlife experiences. From daytime adventures to evening escapades in the city’s vibrant bars and clubs, ensure the groom’s last days of bachelorhood are celebrated in extraordinary fashion.

KTK has been organizing events for groups big and small for well over half a decade. Over these years thousands of Stags and Hen’s have joined in with the crawl. It turns out that partying with young, fun-loving, international crowds and guides in awesome local bars totally checks the box.

There’s no getting lost or sucked into shady or scammy bars. There’s no queuing outside in the cold for hours to get into premium clubs. There are no expensive cover fees for entrance or getting turned away by overly picky bouncers.

And with the crazy amount of experience we’ve had in Poland, from talking to and partying with Stags and Hen’s week-in and week-out, we’ve built up a better insight than anybody as to what makes a mind-blowing, stress-free trip of a lifetime.

We have great connections with hundreds of local companies, which means we can also help plan everything from stepping off the plane to getting back on. There’s nothing too big or small for us to deal with. Airport transfers, accommodation, and a host of different activities are all sorted with a simple email. We’re also extremely flexible and more than happy for people to pick and choose from the various things on offer. To see some examples just scroll below.

Polish Vodka Tasting

When visiting the beautiful city of Kraków, trying out the amazing local vodka is simply a must. The spirit is very different to what you’d expect back home and like whiskey in Ireland or Scotland there’s regional varieties, controversy over the perfect ingredients and whole lot of history and culture that makes the beverage incredibly special. Whether it’s the purest of clear vodkas, ancient herbal infusions or a wild array of finely crafted flavoured varieties; the Poles have it covered.

✓ Includes 7 tastings of the very best of a variety of Polish Vodkas.

✓ Everything from the oldest and most traditional clear vodkas down to the more modern and incredibly tasty flavoured varieties.

✓ A crash course in the history, production, evolution and drinking culture of vodka plus all the funny stories that go with it.

✓ Tasty Polish Tapas snacks to soak up vodka and free softs to clear the palate.

✓ A mini vodka cocktail-making masterclass with a prize cocktail giveaway.

✓ Amazing tips on all the best of Krakow’s fantastic food, drink, activities, and nightlife.

Krakow Beer Hall Feast

Feast in style in Krakow’s oldest brewery-restaurant. Built in to historic underground cellars, the in-house micro-brewery serves up a variety of traditional Austro-Hungarian style beer fresh from tank to tap. Whether you want a stein or a yard of ale, there’s a beer for everyone here.

Equal to the quality of the beer is the restaurant’s famous Polish and Bavarian style mountain food. We’re talking beer roasted pork knuckle, wild boar, huge pork schnitzels, steak, ribs etc etc. It’s even possible to have a whole delicious stuffed and roasted pig for a large group. Alongside its famous selection of meat dishes, there’s plenty of lighter snacks and vegetarian options available.

The restaurant is spacious and air-conditioned, with a style reminiscent to an old Austrian fort. Sports are shown on T.V when available so you don’t have to worry about missing your game. There’s also two private rooms (The Hunting room and the Knights’ Hall) for up to 30 people each. The venue is perfectly located just 5 minutes walk from the center of the main square.

Krakow AK47 Shooting Range Package

Unlike many other activities available abroad, this is something you will simply NEVER be allowed to do back home. Krakow is the ultimate chance to try your aim with the world’s most famous weapon. Our mixed shooting package includes not only the Kalashnikov, but also a Glock pistol, pump-action shotgun, and the Eastern European equivalent of the Uzi sub-machine gun.

It’s a really fun experience and you can enjoy it safe in the knowledge that you’re being taught and watched over by some of the most experienced and professional instructors in the country. You also don’t have to worry about finding your way to the range as door-to-door transport is included in the price.

If there’s any spare time at the range it’s often possible to pay a little extra and have a go on other weapons such as an M4, MP5, or Dessert Eagle. What is it that makes the AK47 so special though? We’ll leave it to none other than Nicholas Cage to explain.

Krakow Go-Karting

Krakow’s Go-Karting arena is a modern, indoor track that’s open all year round. The 8-horsepower Sodi carts are quick and a lot of fun to swing around the technical indoor track. For groups of 15 or more, we can book the entire track and run a Grand Prix-style 3x8min race with a free beer for the winner at the end.
