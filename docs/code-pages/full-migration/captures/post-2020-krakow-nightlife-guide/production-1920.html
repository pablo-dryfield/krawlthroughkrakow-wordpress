Ultimate 2020 Krakow Nightlife Guide

by DPBowman | Apr 5, 2020 | Krakow Guides, Kraków Nightlife, Party Tips

There’s one thing for sure – you can never get bored in Krakow. During the day it has all the monuments, beautiful architecture and history to discover. At night, your own story is waiting to be written. This city never sleeps and there are many places to explore, foods to try and things to experience. No matter what your style of partying is, you will find something suitable. Cozy pubs, countless bars, vibrant clubs, karaoke bars, live music pubs, unusual night and day activities, places with delicious food… Krakow nightlife may be a bit overwhelming. How not to get lost among all the dance floors and bars? Where and when to go during your travel to Krakow? We went through hundreds of spots and picked only the best for you.  

Tips for having a great night out in Krakow

Where to party in Krakow?

Amazing thing about Krakow’s nightlife is that all the places you might want to visit during the night are located nearby. Most crowded and popular zone is the Main Square area. No matter which street you turn into, you will find some bars, restaurants and clubs. If you prefer something less commercial, take a walk to the Kazimierz district. Many spots there have their own unique vibe and are worth visiting.

What is the best time to go out in Krakow? 

When do clubs close in Krakow?

Club parties usually begin around 10 PM and they’re starting to get crowded around midnight. Some clubs however offer various benefits, e.g. free entrance or special prices at the bar for those coming earlier. Night clubs usually close around 4-5 AM. When it comes to the days of the week, you can party literally everyday all year round. The most popular days though are Thursdays (that’s when many students use to go out) and weekends of course, so Friday and Saturday nights.

What to drink while in Krakow?

Your first thought may be vodka and that is absolutely correct. You can drink it mixed with a soft drink or as a shot, you can taste pure vodka, flavoured one or colourful shots. The possibilities are endless so just pick what seems to be the best for you. Don’t want to go too hard? Worry no more! There are many cool cocktail bars where the best bartenders serve some nice drinks. Fancy some beer? That is always a good idea. In Krakow you will find many cheerful places where you can find good choice of craft beers as well.

The best ice breakers to make friends in Krakow

Krakow is a big city, full of students, tourists and international people so you shouldn’t have any problems communicating in English. Polish people, especially the younger generation, can speak English fluently. It is more than likely that the staff in a bar or club that you go to will understand you perfectly. However, if you want to discover the real Krakow nightlife, we recommend you to learn some basic phrases in Polish. In this way, you can make some local friends and experience Polish way of partying. People will surely appreciate your effort and it may bring smiles to a lot of faces. Polish obviously is not the easiest language in the world, but hey, you can do it! 

 

We come with a little help and present you some of the most useful phrases when it comes to partying in Krakow: 

 “Dzień dobry” (jane doh-brih) – Good morning

 “Dobry wieczór” (doh-brih vyeh-choor) – Good evening

 “Jedno piwo proszę” (yed-no pee-voh pro-sheh) – One beer, please

 “Dziękuję” (jen-koo-yeh) – Thank you

 “Przepraszam” (psheh-prah-shahm) – I’m sorry/Excuse me 

 “Na zdrowie!”(nah zdroh-vyeh) – Cheers!

 “Zatańczymy?”(za-tan-chi-mi?) – Shall we dance?

What to avoid while partying in Krakow?

Large selection of places, cheap alcohol and international vibe – your Krakow nightlife experience can be truly unforgettable. But in order to go through the night without any unnecessary problems and to make good memories only, there are few things that you should keep in mind. 

 

#1 Don’t drink in public. 

Drinking in parks, squares and other public spots in Poland is forbidden by law and you can get fine for that. Go to the bar/pub and enjoy your drink there. If you wanna warm up before partying with your own alcohol, better do it before leaving your accommodation. 

 #2 Watch out for your stuff. 

Krakow is a rather safe place to be any time of the day, but you should always be careful cause some thefts can happen. Keep an eye on your belongings in crowded bars and better leave your jacket in a locker room when entering the club. 

 #3 Order alcohol in clubs carefully. 

If you ask the bartender about e.g. vodka soda, he may pick the most expensive brand of alcohol and make you pay for it. It’s not a rule, but it can happen so it’s better to prevent that situation. Ask about the price before choosing your drink and make your order very specific.

 #4 Avoid strip clubs. 

Walking around the Main Square in Krakow you can see promoters inviting people to different places. Some of them can give you discount coupons for drinks or invitations to the clubs. Be careful with the hosts (usually holding umbrellas) inviting to the strip clubs though and don’t go there. Those places are usually oriented just on stealing a drunk client’s money. 

 #5 Be careful with money. 

Paying in foreign currency may be a bit confusing, especially at the beginning or after a few drinks. Try to calculate the prices and don’t pay with the big notes if possible. Having some coins in your pocket makes it easier to pay and to check if you’ve got the right amount of change.  

What are the best places to party in Krakow in 2020?

5 best Pubs in Krakow

Imagine a warm summer evening in Krakow and a cold beer in your hand. Or gloomy weather outside and you sitting in a cozy place with a drink that warms you up. Add some snacks. Doesn’t seem bad, right? The truth is, no matter what time of a year it is, it’s always a good idea to visit Krakow and enjoy a pint of beer served with some typical Polish munchies. Let’s take a look at your best pub options. 

Viva la PINTA

Best pub in Krakow for craft beer lovers

Floriańska 13, Old Town, 31-019 Krakow, Poland

Located right next to the Main Square, Viva la PINTA is a pub opened by a first Polish craft brewery. It offers various beers of their own production as well as other Polish and foreign craft beers. During the summer season you can sit in their garden, in winter you can grab your beer in the spacious basement. All over the year you can try their burgers, wraps and sandwiches.

Wezze Krafta

Great craft beer place with a hipster vibe

Dolnych Młynów 10/3, 31-124 Krakow, Poland

Great selection of beers in a popular and very lively place. Professional bartenders know everything about crafted beer and are happy to share their knowledge with anyone interested. Industrial, yet cozy interior and frequent beer events are what makes that place unique.

House of Beer

Pub with delicious food and wide selection of beers

Swietego Tomasza 35, Old Town, 31-027 Krakow, Poland

Over 200 bottles and 21 taps with beer over two bars. Modern pub dishes, e.g. pulled pork sandwich or hot buffalo wings as well as food that can be ordered to share with friends. House of Beer has its own atmosphere which allows the guests to really enjoy their food and drinks.

5 best Bars in Krakow

No matter if you fancy a cocktail, beer or few vodka shots, Krakow’s got you covered. There are hundreds of bars in the city. Pay attention to typical shot bars – they are usually crowded and serve cheap alcohol. These places are where the Krakow nightlife experience typically either starts or ends.

Camouflaged bar with high quality cocktails

Secret location, Kazimierz district, Krakow, Poland

This is a real hidden gem of Krakow nightlife. To find this bar full of original and delicious cocktails, you have to follow instructions on their website. While playing a game and looking for the address, you get the chance to discover the beauty of Kazimierz district. The entrance to the bar is hidden in the bathroom of another club and thanks to its character, it’s not the most touristic spot. Definitely worth checking out.

3 best Karaoke bars in Krakow

Discovering Krakow nightlife makes you so happy that you wanna sing? Or you’re just looking for an alternative way of spending an evening? Hop on the stage and show your vocal skills or just sing in the background while sipping your drink. It doesn’t really matter if you can sing or not, it’s all about having fun!

Mr. Vavelsky Pub

Karaoke bar, pub & garden in the heart of Krakow

Gołebia 6, Old Town, 30-007 Krakow, Poland

Original interior with a real dragon flying under the ceiling, charming summer garden, good food and variety of drinks. Friendly atmosphere in this place makes it easy to grab the mic and sing your heart out. Besides karaoke, the bar organizes many different events, e.g. pub quizzes or stand up comedy evenings.

3 best Live music bars/pubs

Krakow is a city of culture and many artists. Wanna go to some place where good live music is played? You couldn’t find better! Every evening various concerts take place in different bars and pubs in town, you just need to know where to look for them.

Alchemia

Important music events in cozy basement

Estery 5, Kazimierz, 31-056 Krakow, Poland

One of the many magical places in Kazimierz, but it is Alchemia where the most cultural events happen. Extraordinary interior, stylized as a crazy Alchemist’s laboratory and a snugly cellar where all the events and concerts take place. The club hosts thousands of artists, also international, especially during events such as the Jewish Culture Festival or the Krakow Jazz Autumn.

ZetPeTe

Progressive community centre where art and culture meet

Dolnych Młynów 10, Old Town, 31-124 Krakow, Poland

It’s definitely an interesting and alternative place, the real Krakow nightlife cultural space and something different that’s worth visiting. You’ll find concerts with various types of music there, from folk to hop-hop, as well as other art exhibitions and performances.

3 best Places where to eat after a night out in Krakow

Exploring Krakow nightlife can be exhausting. After hours of moving between different bars, dancing on various dance floors and a couple of drinks, you will probably realize that you’re getting hungry. First thought can be just grabbing some kebab or walking to the nearest fast food restaurant. But hold on for a moment, you didn’t come all the way to this city to eat like at home, right? How to finish the night or recharge batteries Polish style? 

Przypiecek

Polish pierogi 24/7

Sławkowska 32, Old Town, 31-014 Krakow, Poland

Can you imagine something better than a place where pierogi are served 24/7? Sounds like heaven and tastes just as good. Almost 20 various types of that popular Polish dish, soups and breakfast options are waiting right next to the Main Square to save all the weary travelers, whether hunger catches them during the day or in the middle of the night.

Kiełbaski z Niebieskiej Nyski

Traditional sausages roasted on fire

Grzegórzecka 4, Kazimierz, 33-332 Krakow, Poland

Iconic place on the map of Krakow night snacks and probably the oldest food truck in the city. It may seem nothing special, but tasting Polish sausage grilled on fire in front of a blue retro van is something hard to experience anywhere else. The taste and quality of the products have remained unchanged for years and the place is often called ‘the real taste of Krakow’.

Okraglak

Best of Polish Zapiekanka

Estery 46, Kazimierz, 31-056 Krakow, Poland

It’s almost impossible to visit Krakow and not hear about the famous Zapiekanka. It’s a long bread, topped with mushrooms, cheese, ham, veggies or anything you can possibly imagine. If you want to try them after the eventful night, Kazimierz is the place to go. In the heart of the district you will find a round building with several bars serving many Zapiekanka options, so simply choose one that suits you best.
