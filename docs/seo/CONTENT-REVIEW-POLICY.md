# KTK Content Review Policy

Owner-approved on 14 November 2026: **monthly KTK-team reviews, plus seasonal
checks before launch and after the event**. This is a working process, not an
installed reminder, scheduled automation or external account change.

## Responsibilities

- **KTK team:** owns business facts, recommendations, offers, operational rules,
  review-count snapshots and final approval. Confirm corrections in this project
  conversation; a coding agent must not invent missing details.
- **Code maintainer:** records those confirmations and primary sources, updates
  the canonical editable inputs, runs checks and publishes staging for review.
  Do not manually patch captures, generated HTML or compiled assets.
- **Specialist:** reviews medical or other high-stakes claims when needed. The
  alcohol-health article remains explicitly on hold; general editorial approval
  does not authorize its rewrite, deletion, redirect or noindex.
- **Production approver:** the owner separately approves the reviewed staging
  release. This policy does not authorize production deployment or changes to
  prices, availability, orders, consent, account settings or the legacy store.

Use roles rather than inventing a named staff member. KTK may nominate an
individual later without changing ownership of this process.

## Confirmed Enquiry Rules

The owner confirmed on 14 November that the team responds after seeing an
enquiry by email. Do not publish a fixed response-time guarantee or imply
round-the-clock monitoring. This confirms the working process, not tested SMTP
delivery or permission to send real test enquiries.

Group minimums, private arrangements, booking lead times and deposit/cancellation
terms are confirmed individually in each quote. Do not invent standard numbers
or terms. Contact and the stag/hen/student introductions now state these rules
on staging, verified14 November. Seven older stag/hen activity modules have also
been reviewed on staging; their original media and enquiry/booking controls are
retained. Remaining activity details and production copy have not all been
reviewed or promoted; the isolated production endpoint repair is a separate
release.

## Games Copy And Brand Tone

The owner confirmed on14 November that KTK Drunken Jenga should be presented as
a fun KTK activity alongside the other games. Do not substitute a regular-Jenga
recommendation or add optional-participation wording to these guides. Preserve
the useful KTK rule-sheet downloads and authentic branding; do not restore
instructions that encourage excessive or compulsory consumption.

This is an editorial preference, not a consent-control change. There is no
identified automatic organic-search penalty simply for the game's name. Do not
present an agent's tone preference as a Google requirement or confuse separate
Google Ads alcohol policies with organic ranking. The games release records
the checked primary guidance and exact staging scope.

Complete at least one review each calendar month. Also check a campaign before
promotion starts, before material changes, and immediately after its agreed
cutoff. A review records what was actually checked, not a blanket fresh date for
every page.

## Change Record And Release

1. Record the review date, KTK confirmation or official source, exact affected
   pages/fields and unresolved questions in the scoped review document.
2. Keep a pre-edit checkpoint and update only canonical source inputs. Preserve
   original URLs, publication/byline history, media and unrelated content unless
   the owner explicitly approves a change.
3. Build and test the affected behavior, protected boundaries and staging
   noindex/canonical policy. Keep failed diagnostics alongside the correction.
4. Deploy only within the approved scope, verify origin checks, purge only the
   authorized staging cache scope and verify normal desktop/mobile responses.
5. Update both roadmap views with evidence. Mark a whole workstream complete
   only when its acceptance criteria are met, not because a checklist exists.
6. Obtain separate owner approval before any production release. Record the
   actual release date; do not claim an unpublished staging change is live.
