## Proposed approach without a universal expiry

Retain records while they are needed for the relevant request, recruitment, competition, necessary legal obligation or properly assessed claim purpose. Review at the end of that purpose and establish a proportionate review limit; do not retain an entire message merely because one transaction fact needs to remain. An exception needs an evidenced basis and necessary scope. There is no universal statutory number supplied by this review.

The exact proposed [retention clause](REPLACEMENT-CLAUSES.md) preserves this principle without claiming that reviews or deletion already happen. The owner has not supplied a review cadence or a documented cross-system disposal process. These remain implementation/operational gaps, rather than an unperformed legal-content audit.

| Record group | Purpose / review trigger | Remaining decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Active enquiries | Handle the customer's request and agreed follow-up; review when that purpose ends | How closure is recorded and how soon a retention review occurs |
| Closed enquiries without a booking | Review whether any continuing purpose or documented obligation justifies retention | A justified expiry/review interval and approved deletion handling |
| Enquiries leading to bookings/disputes | Distinguish necessary transaction/claim records from an entire enquiry copy | Applicable obligations and which specific records must be retained; commerce records are not changed by this work |
| Job applications | Handle the relevant recruitment; review at the end of that process | Retention basis/interval and any separate arrangement for future recruitment |
| Competition entries | Judge the relevant monthly round, contact the winner and administer the prize | Treatment of unsuccessful/old entries, prize records and separately permitted publication |
| Privacy requests | Handle requests and any necessary record of response | Responsible KTK-team process, verification, exceptions and the response record's retention |

The schedule must cover OmniLodge submitted fields, immutable notification snapshots, notes and audit history; corresponding Gmail copies; any exports; and relevant backup/provider processes. Closing a record is not deletion. Current inbox controls and rollback guards deliberately retain data; no purge endpoint or auto-expiry is implemented here. Define a reviewed technical process before claiming that a policy is enforced.

Use only the data needed for the stated purpose. The European Commission explains that retention/review limits should reflect the purpose and applicable obligations. This does not prescribe a universal period for KTK's enquiries or applications.
