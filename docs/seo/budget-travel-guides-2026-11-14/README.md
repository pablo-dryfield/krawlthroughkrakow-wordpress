## Primary Sources Checked

Checked 14 November 2026. These are visitor-policy references, not permission to
republish operator photos or copy their whole guides.

- [Wawel visitor information](https://wawel.krakow.pl/en/explore): exhibitions,
  ticketing and visit limits. [Free November 2026](https://wawel.krakow.pl/en/free-november-2026)
  is a temporary, limited campaign, not an evergreen all-exhibitions promise.
- [City Royal Route](https://www.krakow.pl/turystyka/1218,artykul,droga-krolewska.html):
  the central route through Florian Gate, Florianska, the square, Grodzka and
  Kanonicza towards Wawel. Its old publication date does not establish current
  interior hours; only the route geography is used.
- [Wawel Cathedral visitor information](https://www.katedra-wawelska.pl/en/katedra-wawelska/zaplanuj-wizyte/):
  tourist admission is ticketed separately from the castle.
- [Rynek Underground](https://muzeumkrakowa.pl/en/branches/rynek-underground):
  Tuesday free-admission tickets are limited, collected on the day and cannot
  be reserved; check closures before travel.
- [ZTP ticket guide](https://ztp.krakow.pl/en/kmk-public-transport/kmk-ticket-guide):
  duration/zone and concession eligibility matter; the combined ticket excludes
  Krakow Airport. First direct web fetch timed out; search extraction remains
  available. Recheck before publishing specific fares.
- [Airport buses](https://krakowairport.pl/en/passenger/transporten/directions/from-to-krakow-airport)
  and [airport trains](https://krakowairport.pl/en/passenger/transport-en/directions/from-to-krakow-airport/train-en/en):
  separate route/ticket choices and bus-ticket validation.
- [LajkBike](https://ztp.krakow.pl/rower/lajkbike) is long-term rental, not the
  old short-stay Wavelo scheme. [Park-e-Bike](https://ztp.krakow.pl/rower/park-e-bike)
  has account/eligibility, seasonal and return restrictions; do not promise all
  overseas weekend visitors a free bike.
