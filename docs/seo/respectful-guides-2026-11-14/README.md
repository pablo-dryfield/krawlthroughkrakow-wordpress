- Keep prank-planning intent, but replace coercion, body shaming, public
  humiliation and DIY staged-abduction instructions with opt-in alternatives.
  Do not claim a particular activity is a verified KTK package or risk-free.
- Make the hostel title's seven practical tips real and distinct. Replace
  unassessed snoring-device recommendations with considerate room planning;
  do not diagnose or prescribe. Household rules and room arrangements vary.
- Keep useful date-planning advice without promising romance. Make boundaries,
  personal choice, privacy and an independent journey home explicit. Repair
  the eleven contents links that currently lead to an old Google document.
- Use native, collapsible contents while keeping the article sections readable
  without JavaScript. Preserve old unique fragment IDs; remove only duplicated
  generated list IDs, not useful destinations.
- Retain original photographs and their wrappers/links. No generated imagery,
  provider embed, price, new event, guaranteed admission or invented package.
