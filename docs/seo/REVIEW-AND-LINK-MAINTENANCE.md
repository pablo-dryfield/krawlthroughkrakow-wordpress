## Reviews: A Concrete Starting Process

KTK owns the post-experience request and responses; the code maintainer owns
website links and dated snapshots. Use the agreed monthly and pre/post seasonal
review cadence in [the content policy](CONTENT-REVIEW-POLICY.md). Nominate an
actual team member before treating this as an operating process.

1. Offer the same optional, neutral request to genuine customers through an
   existing approved channel. Include unhappy customers; do not use a private
   satisfaction survey to decide who receives a public-review link. Do not
   reward reviews, ask for a particular rating or wording, require staff names,
   pressure guests to review on site, or set staff review quotas. These choices
   follow [Google's current review policy](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114?hl=en).
2. Keep the request separate from booking eligibility, refunds, complaints and
   discount decisions. Do not change contact consent, recipients, customer
   records or deferred consent work to implement this draft.
3. For GetYourGuide bookings, use its native review process. Its supplier guide
   says that it sends the invitation and two reminders; an additional KTK email
   is unnecessary. This does not create a KTK mail campaign or confirm account
   settings. [GetYourGuide supplier guidance](https://supply.getyourguide.support/hc/en-us/articles/13981000922141-Understanding-Customer-Reviews-on-GetYourGuide).
4. Read feedback and respond usefully through the platform's owner account.
   Address the actual issue without publishing booking references or personal
   details. Do not promise a refund, investigation result or reply deadline in a
   reusable template. Escalate operational issues to the KTK team.
5. Check the three existing platform destinations and record any replacement
   count/rating with its source and date before changing the website. The source
   remains `staging/wp-content/mu-plugins/ktk-homepage-assets/reviews.php`.
   Existing rounded figures stay dated snapshots; no live totals or copied
   review quotations are introduced. The native Testimonials fallback uses
   those same links and preserves the separate consent-gated Shapo embed.

Suggested request text, ready for owner review:

> Thank you for joining Krawl Through Krakow. If you'd like to share your
> experience, please leave an honest review on Google or Tripadvisor using the
> links below. All feedback is welcome.

Insert the existing Google/Tripadvisor listing URLs from the shared source;
verify the recipient and channel permissions before use. On16 November2026,
[Tripadvisor's public review guidelines](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Trust-lvBd3L1aU38Y.html)
were retrieved normally. Guests write their own firsthand accounts. Do not
write for them, suggest review wording, watch submission or exchange drinks,
discounts or contest entry for reviews of any rating. Do not reward employees
for review volume or name mentions, pressure reviews/removals or route negative
feedback away from the public review path. The neutral invitation above does
not prescribe what a guest should write.
