## Proposed article copy

### Halloween in Krakow: plan your visit and a night out

Planning a Halloween trip to Krakow? Start with the dates you will be in the city and the published details of any event you want to join. This guide helps you separate your costume-party plans from the remembrance traditions of early November and decide what to check before booking a night out.

### Halloween and All Saints’ Day

Halloween falls on 31 October. All Saints’ Day, or Wszystkich Świętych, follows on 1 November. In Krakow, families visit the graves of loved ones, light candles and leave flowers. Cemetery visits are occasions for remembrance; plan your nightlife and any visit to a cemetery with that distinction in mind.

Check current city travel information before setting out during this period. Your route and return journey should be part of your plans, especially when you are joining friends at an unfamiliar meeting point.

### Check the published KTK Halloween details

For Krawl Through Krakow, use the [Halloween event page](https://krawlthroughkrakow.com/staging/store2/halloween-pub-crawl-51/) for published dates, meeting instructions, ticket prices and inclusions. Read the information for the date you want to attend before making a reservation.

Keep these questions together when comparing your plans:

- What date and starting time are advertised?
- Where is the meeting point, and how will you get there?
- What does the ticket include, and what conditions apply?
- What venue, costume or activity details are actually confirmed?
- How will you get back to your accommodation?

If the published details do not answer your question, [contact the KTK team](https://krawlthroughkrakow.com/staging/contact/) before booking. For a group enquiry, share your intended date, group size and the experience you are looking for so the team can discuss the arrangements with you.

### Choose a costume you can enjoy wearing

A costume can be simple or elaborate: a classic monster, a character you like, a coordinated group theme or something you have made yourself. Think about how it will feel over the whole evening, including walking between places and travelling back afterwards.

Check the relevant venue or event guidance before bringing masks or props. Give yourself time to gather what you need, and agree a meeting plan with friends if you are arriving separately.

### Read guest reviews

Read guest reviews of Krawl Through Krakow on [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attraction_Review-g274772-d2725527-Reviews-Krawl_Through_Krakow_Pub_Crawl-Krakow_Lesser_Poland_Province_Southern_Poland.html) and [GetYourGuide](https://www.getyourguide.com/krakow-l40/krakow-pub-crawl-1h-open-bar-vip-entry-welcome-shots-t443425/). These are general pub-crawl listings. Use the published Halloween event details when deciding what is included in the event you are considering.

### Before your night out

Have the meeting instructions and your return route to hand. Check the current ticket information, agree your plans with friends and ask the team about anything that remains unclear. With those practical details settled, you can focus on choosing your costume and enjoying your time in Krakow.
