## Polish Vodka Tasting

Interested in a Polish vodka tasting for your hen group? Send your preferred date, group size and the kind of experience you would enjoy.

Ask about the venue, session length, number and size of samples, snacks and options for non-drinkers. Confirm the format, any participation requirements and the exact inclusions in your individual quote before adding it to your plans. Use the hen-group enquiry form below to share your preferences.

## Group Transport

For group transport, share your pickup and destination, dates and times, passenger count, luggage and any access requirements. Say whether you are asking about an airport transfer or a journey during your stay.

Your individual quote should identify the vehicle, passenger capacity, journey or hire duration, total price and any agreed extras. Confirm those details before arranging the rest of the group’s schedule. Use the enquiry form below to describe the transport you need.

## Cocktail Making Class

Interested in a cocktail-making session? Tell us your preferred date, group size and the type of session you would enjoy, including whether anyone would prefer non-alcoholic drinks.

Ask about the venue, instructor, duration, ingredients, equipment, drinks and any private arrangements. Confirm the workshop format and inclusions in your individual quote. Send your preferences through the hen-group enquiry form below.

## Spa Day / Beauty Treatment

If you would like to include a spa or beauty appointment in the weekend, tell us which options interest your group, your preferred date and the number of guests.

Ask about available treatments, the venue, appointment lengths, access arrangements and the provider’s requirements. Confirm the quoted services, price and terms before planning around an appointment. Use the enquiry form below to explain your preferences.

## Polish Food Tour

For a food-focused group outing, tell us whether you are interested in tastings, a meal or a walk with food stops. Share your preferred date, group size and any dietary, allergy or access needs you want to discuss.

Ask about the venues, food and drinks, route, walking time and how the venues can address your requirements. Confirm the itinerary, inclusions and total in your individual quote before making plans. Send your preferences through the hen-group enquiry form below.
