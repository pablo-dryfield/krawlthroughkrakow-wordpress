establishes overlap, not proven harmful cannibalization or a reason to301 a URL.
Examples below are exact returned query rows, not all-query or sitewide metrics.

## Planning Inference

Keep the established city guide as a useful planning destination, with a clear
route to the current offer. Keep `/new-years-eve/` as the operator's event/sales
page with confirmed facts, groups and booking. The secondary guide needs a
distinct, useful purpose or a later consolidation assessment including content,
links and historical context; lower observed traffic alone is not deletion proof.
No canonical, URL, redirect or indexing decision was made in this review.

These overlapping windows are not additive and span different seasonal demand.
Most of each window predates the5 November full migration. They cannot establish
migration harm/benefit or a staging improvement's effect on rankings/conversions.
Broader M02 pair/cohort work and production follow-up remain open.
